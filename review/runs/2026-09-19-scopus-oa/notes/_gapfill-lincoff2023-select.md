# Semaglutide and Cardiovascular Outcomes in Obesity without Diabetes (SELECT)

- **Citation:** Lincoff AM, et al. N Engl J Med. 2023;389:2221-32. doi:10.1056/NEJMoa2307563
- **Source file:** papers/scopus-oa-gapfill/Lincoff2023_307563.pdf
- **Extracted:** 2026-09-19
- **Screening:** Gap-fill for G5 (not in the original 10-record Scopus export)

## Research question
Does weekly subcutaneous semaglutide 2.4 mg reduce major cardiovascular events versus placebo in people with preexisting CVD and overweight/obesity but without diabetes?

## Methods
Multicenter double-blind randomized placebo-controlled event-driven superiority trial (Abstract).

## Sample / data
N=17,604 (8803 semaglutide / 8801 placebo); age ≥45; BMI ≥27; preexisting CVD; no diabetes history (Abstract). Mean follow-up 39.8±9.4 months. NCT03574597.

## Key findings
- Primary composite (CV death, nonfatal MI, or nonfatal stroke): 569/8803 (6.5%) vs 701/8801 (8.0%); HR 0.80 (95% CI 0.72–0.90) (Abstract).
- Permanent discontinuation for AEs: 16.6% vs 8.2% (Abstract).

## Limitations
Funded by Novo Nordisk. Not a mechanistic PCSK5 study; not a diabetes outcomes trial.
