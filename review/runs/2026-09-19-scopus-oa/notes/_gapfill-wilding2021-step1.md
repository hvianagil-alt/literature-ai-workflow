# Once-Weekly Semaglutide in Adults with Overweight or Obesity (STEP 1)

- **Citation:** Wilding JPH, et al. N Engl J Med. 2021;384:989-1002. doi:10.1056/NEJMoa2032183
- **Source file:** papers/scopus-oa-gapfill/Wilding2021_032183.pdf
- **Extracted:** 2026-09-19
- **Screening:** Gap-fill for G1 (not in the original 10-record Scopus export)

## Research question
Does once-weekly subcutaneous semaglutide 2.4 mg plus lifestyle intervention reduce body weight versus placebo in adults with overweight or obesity without diabetes?

## Methods
Double-blind RCT; 2:1 randomization; 68 weeks; coprimary endpoints percentage weight change and ≥5% weight reduction (Abstract).

## Sample / data
N=1961; BMI ≥30, or ≥27 with ≥1 weight-related comorbidity; no diabetes (Abstract). STEP 1; NCT03548935.

## Key findings
- Mean weight change week 68: −14.9% (semaglutide) vs −2.4% (placebo); estimated treatment difference −12.4 percentage points (95% CI −13.4 to −11.5) (Abstract).
- Absolute weight: −15.3 kg vs −2.6 kg; difference −12.7 kg (95% CI −13.7 to −11.7) (Abstract).
- GI events were the most common AEs; discontinuation for GI events 4.5% vs 0.8% (Abstract).

## Limitations
Funded by Novo Nordisk. Obesity/overweight without diabetes — not prediabetes (Wu) and not a delivery study.

## Relevance
Gap-fill for putting Wu’s prediabetes NMA weight MD in perspective. Different population and endpoint (% vs kg MD vs placebo in a single trial).
