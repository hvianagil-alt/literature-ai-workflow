# Microneedle patches deliver targeted GLP-1RAs-loaded nanoparticles for the treatment of rheumatoid arthritis

- **Citation (as given in the paper / filename):** Zhang H, liu Y, Zhang S, Wang Z, Zhang X, Zhang Y, Zhang J, Liang S, Li C, Su M, Tian Z, Luo L. J Nanobiotechnology. 2026;24:265. doi:10.1186/s12951-026-04261-0
- **Source file:** papers/scopus-oa/Zhang2026.pdf
- **Extracted:** 2026-09-19
- **Screening:** Included (GLP-1 RA + nanoparticle/microneedle delivery; inflammatory joint disease)

## Research question
Can a cartilage-targeted silk-fibroin nanoparticle loaded with GLP-1 RAs, delivered by dissolving microneedles, improve articular exposure and RA pathology, and do GLP-1 RAs act on chondrocyte ferroptosis via cGAS-STING?

## Methods
Hierarchical delivery: silk fibroin nanoparticles modified with type II collagen-binding peptide WYRGRL (GLP-1RAs@NPs-WYRGRL), loaded into methacrylated hyaluronic acid dissolving microneedles. In vitro chondrocyte/macrophage assays; collagen-induced arthritis (CIA) rat model; HPLC for encapsulation/release (Methods/Results).

## Sample / data
CIA rats used for joint targeting and efficacy; n=6 per group is stated for some biodistribution arms (figure legends). Exact n for every efficacy arm is not uniformly restated in extractable text.

## Key findings
- Encapsulation efficiency of GLP-1RAs@NPs was approximately 28% (silk:GLP-1 RAs mass ratio 5:1) and 33% (10:1), without a statistically significant difference between those ratios (Results; Fig. 4H).
- About 60% of GLP-1 RAs was released by day 14 (Results; Fig. 4I).
- Authors report the composite MN system accumulated in arthritic joints, inhibited chondrocyte ferroptosis via suppression of cGAS-STING, and promoted macrophage M2 polarization, attenuating RA progression (Abstract).
- They frame inefficient articular delivery after systemic GLP-1 RA dosing as the clinical gap this device is meant to address (Abstract; Introduction).

## Limitations (as stated by the authors, or evident from the methods)
- Preclinical (CIA rat / cell) only; no human RA trial in this paper.
- Which specific GLP-1 RA molecule was loaded is not clearly named in the extracted abstract (referred to as “GLP-1RAs”).
- Open Access CC BY-NC-ND.

## Relevance to our research question
Directly on-topic for GLP-1 RA **drug delivery** (microneedle + targeted nanoparticles). Disease is RA, not diabetes; protocol allowed closely related inflammatory settings.

## Open questions / things to verify
