# Engineering Bioactive Self-Healing Antibacterial Exosomes Hydrogel for Promoting Chronic Diabetic Wound Healing and Complete Skin Regeneration

- **Citation (as given in the paper / filename):** Wang C, Wang M, Xu T, Zhang X, Lin C, Gao W, Xu H, Lei B, Mao C. Theranostics. 2019;9(1):65-76. doi:10.7150/thno.29766
- **Source file:** papers/scopus-oa-gapfill/Wang2018_29766.pdf
- **Extracted:** 2026-09-19
- **Screening:** Gap-fill **G4** (other AMSC-exosome hydrogels for diabetic wounds vs Chen 2026). Included after OA PDF retrieved.

## Research question
Can a multifunctional FHE hydrogel with sustained adipose MSC exosome (AMSCs-exo) release improve chronic diabetic full-thickness wound healing versus exosomes or hydrogel alone?

## Methods
FHE hydrogel (Pluronic F127 / oxidative hyaluronic acid / poly-ε-L-lysine) loaded with AMSCs-exo; in vitro HUVEC assays; in vivo diabetic full-thickness cutaneous wounds, four groups observed on days 0, 3, 7, 14, 21 (Abstract; Results).

## Sample / data
In vivo diabetic mice (Results: “Gross observation of wound closure in mice”). Four groups compared. Western blot of exo markers n=3 (figure legend). **Exact n per in-vivo wound group is not stated** in the extracted text.

## Key findings
- FHE@exo promoted HUVEC proliferation, migration, and tube formation vs controls in vitro (Abstract).
- In vivo, FHE@exo enhanced wound closure, angiogenesis, re-epithelialization, and collagen deposition vs exosomes or FHE hydrogel alone (Abstract).
- Authors report better healing than either component alone, i.e. synergy of sustained exo release plus hydrogel (Abstract).
- Skin appendages and less scar tissue reported in FHE@exo-treated wounds (Abstract).

## Limitations (as stated by the authors, or evident from the methods)
- Preclinical mouse diabetic wounds, not a human trial.
- Animal group n not recovered from extract — do not invent n.
- Multi-component device (as with Chen 2026).

## Relevance to our research question
Same product **class** as Chen 2026 (adipose MSC exosomes in a hydrogel for diabetic skin wounds). Supports that this design family is an established preclinical strategy. Does **not** supply Chen’s missing group n, and is not GLP-1.
