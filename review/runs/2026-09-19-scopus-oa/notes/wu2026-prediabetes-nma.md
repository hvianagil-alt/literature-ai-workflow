# Efficacy and safety of anti-prediabetic drugs in patients with prediabetes: a Bayesian network meta-analysis

- **Citation (as given in the paper / filename):** Wu Y, Wang Z, Tuersun A, Yu Q, Zhong Y, Ali SSA, Liu H, Hu X, Zhang Y, Pang L, Li L, Gao L, Wu Q, Wang S, Cui M, Sun L, Wu Y, Yin A, Zhang L, Ma G. BMC Med. 2026;24:174. doi:10.1186/s12916-026-04705-2
- **Source file:** papers/scopus-oa/Wu2026.pdf
- **Extracted:** 2026-09-19
- **Screening:** Included (network meta-analysis of drugs including GLP-1 RAs and tirzepatide in prediabetes)

## Research question
Among anti-prediabetic drugs, which have the best efficacy and safety versus placebo and each other for adults with prediabetes?

## Methods
Bayesian network meta-analysis of RCTs (≥12 weeks) in adults with prediabetes. Databases: PubMed, Embase, Cochrane Library, Web of Science, ClinicalTrials.gov, searched before 29 March 2025. PROSPERO CRD42025636991 (Abstract; Methods).

## Sample / data
55 RCTs; 37 interventions; 16,610 participants (Abstract; Results). Identification started at 6663 records; 3516 duplicates removed (Results).

## Key findings
- Versus placebo, most drugs reduced HbA1c (MD −0.94 to −0.27%), FPG (MD −26.42 to −0.15 mg/dL), weight (MD −13.59 to −5.99 kg), and BMI (MD −4.50 to −0.08 kg/m²) (Abstract).
- Semaglutide 2.4 mg SC: largest weight-loss MD among those highlighted (−13.59 kg; 95% CI −17.30 to −9.91) and HbA1c MD −0.39% (95% CI −0.55 to −0.25) (Abstract).
- Tirzepatide 15 mg: FPG MD −9.58 mg/dL (95% CI −12.00 to −7.15) (Abstract).
- Authors’ conclusion: GLP-1 RAs, GIP/GLP-1 RAs, and TZDs had favorable efficacy; 2.4 mg semaglutide SC and 15 mg tirzepatide were “the best option” among included interventions for glucose and BMI control (Abstract/Conclusions).
- No significant difference in overall AE incidence among interventions; sitagliptin 100 mg had higher SAEs in their analysis (Abstract).

## Limitations (as stated by the authors)
Limited RCTs per drug class; small samples and heterogeneity/publication bias for some outcomes; follow-up 12–120 weeks; safety outcomes often missing; some interventions very small n (Limitations).

## Relevance to our research question
The only **clinical comparative-effectiveness** paper in this export for GLP-1/GIP-GLP-1 drugs (prediabetes, not a delivery study).
