# PCSK5 promotes angiogenesis and cardiac repair after myocardial infarction

- **Citation (as given in the paper / filename):** Guo J, Ma S, Ma J, Liu M, Ge F, Wang X, Lin J, Jiang N, Lin X, Lian Z, Zuo Z, Li L, Cao G, Li Y, Wei X, NG X, Zhi X, Osto E, Dai Y, Li J, Wang L, Jiang H, Meng D. Nat Commun. 2026;17. doi:10.1038/s41467-026-72148-7
- **Source file:** papers/scopus-oa/Guo2026.pdf
- **Extracted:** 2026-09-19
- **Screening:** Included after full text (title is PCSK5/MI; body reports semaglutide-driven angiogenesis partly via PCSK5)

## Research question
Does endothelial PCSK5 promote post-MI angiogenesis and repair, and does semaglutide act through this pathway?

## Methods
Human LV and plasma (MI vs controls); mouse MI models including endothelial *Pcsk5* knockout; HUVEC assays; semaglutide treatment in cells and mice (Introduction/Results). GEO datasets GSE132143 (human) and GSE184986 (mouse) used to nominate PCSK5 (Results).

## Sample / data
Examples stated in figure legends: plasma PCSK5 ELISA controls n=30 vs AMI n=28; several mouse histology/WB arms n=6 per group; AMI correlation cohort n=42 for PCSK5/VEGFA (and GLP-1 in later panels). Heart qPCR: n=5 per group in one human panel.

## Key findings
- PCSK5 was among 104 genes upregulated post-MI in both human and mouse datasets; protein/mRNA higher in MI hearts and plasma (Results).
- Endothelial *Pcsk5* loss impaired post-MI repair/angiogenesis in mice (Results figure legends).
- “Additionally, semaglutide promoted angiogenesis and tissue repair, with its effects being partially mediated by PCSK5 in mice post-MI” (Introduction summary of the study).
- Semaglutide increased PCSK5 via ETS1 in HUVECs; PCSK5 knockdown attenuated semaglutide’s angiogenic gene program (Results, semaglutide subsection).
- Authors report a positive correlation between plasma PCSK5 and GLP-1 in patients with MI (Results).

## Limitations (as stated by the authors, or evident from the methods)
- Mechanistic cardiology paper; not a diabetes RCT.
- Human plasma n is modest (tens, not hundreds).
- Semaglutide is a supporting mechanism, not the sole focus of the title.

## Relevance to our research question
Links a **GLP-1 RA (semaglutide)** to cardiac repair biology. Not a formulation/delivery paper.
