# The Discovery and Development of Liraglutide and Semaglutide

- **Citation (as given in the paper / filename):** Knudsen LB, Lau J. Front Endocrinol. 2019;10:155. doi:10.3389/fendo.2019.00155
- **Source file:** papers/scopus-oa-gapfill/Knudsen2019_00155.pdf
- **Extracted:** 2026-09-19
- **Screening:** Gap-fill **G2** (independent source for oral semaglutide bioavailability vs Jeong’s product-literature <2% remark). Included after OA PDF retrieved.

## Research question
How were liraglutide and semaglutide discovered and developed, including the oral SNAC formulation of semaglutide?

## Methods
Narrative review by Novo Nordisk authors of peptide design, clinical development, and oral absorption strategy (title page; oral-semaglutide section).

## Sample / data
Not a new trial. Cites phase 2 clinical data for oral semaglutide + SNAC (N-[8-(2-hydroxybenzoyl)amino] caprylate).

## Key findings
- Orally administered semaglutide is a tablet with the absorption enhancer SNAC (oral-semaglutide section).
- “Phase 2 clinical data showed that, while bioavailability was low following oral administration (∼1%), it led to the same efficacy in terms of both glycemic control and weight loss as subcutaneously delivered semaglutide” (oral-semaglutide section; they cite reference 255 in that paper).
- Absorption is described as localized in the stomach with SNAC (scintigraphy discussion in that section).

## Limitations (as stated by the authors, or evident from the methods)
- Review, not a head-to-head PK trial vs pulmonary liraglutide.
- Industry-authored discovery narrative.
- The ∼1% figure is attributed to phase 2 data cited inside this review, not re-analysed here.

## Relevance to our research question
Independent OA full text supporting **low oral semaglutide bioavailability (~1%)**, in the same direction as Jeong 2026’s product-literature contrast (<2%). Still not an oral arm in Jeong’s rats, and not a clinical comparison with inhalable liraglutide.
