# Engineering Bioactive Self-Healing Antibacterial Exosomes Hydrogel for Promoting Chronic Diabetic Wound Healing

- **Citation:** Wang C, et al. Theranostics. 2019;9(1):65-76. doi:10.7150/thno.29766
- **Source file:** papers/scopus-oa-gapfill/Wang2018_29766.pdf
- **Extracted:** 2026-09-19
- **Screening:** Gap-fill for G4 (not in the original 10-record Scopus export)

## Research question
Does an injectable self-healing antibacterial polypeptide hydrogel releasing adipose-MSC exosomes improve diabetic full-thickness wound healing?

## Methods
FHE hydrogel (F127/OHA-EPL) with pH-responsive AMSC-exosome release; in vitro HUVECs; in vivo diabetic full-thickness cutaneous wounds (Abstract).

## Sample / data
Diabetic wound model in vivo; HUVECs in vitro. Exact animal n not copied from this extract.

## Key findings
- Hydrogel described as self-healing, injectable, antibacterial, with long-term pH-responsive exosome release (Abstract).
- FHE@exo promoted HUVEC proliferation, migration, and tube formation in vitro (Abstract).
- In vivo, authors report enhanced wound closure, faster angiogenesis, and (per abstract) complete skin regeneration claims (Abstract).

## Limitations
Preclinical; 2019 device distinct from Chen 2026’s spirulina/laser combination. Abstract “complete skin regeneration” is the authors’ claim.

## Relevance
Second MSC-exosome hydrogel diabetic-wound paper. Supports that Chen 2026 is not a unique construct, but does not supply Chen’s missing group n.
