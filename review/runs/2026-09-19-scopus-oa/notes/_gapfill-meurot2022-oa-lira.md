# Liraglutide … analgesic, anti-inflammatory and anti-degradative actions in osteoarthritis

- **Citation:** Meurot C, et al. Sci Rep. 2022;12:1567. doi:10.1038/s41598-022-05323-7
- **Source file:** papers/scopus-oa-gapfill/Meurot2022_53237.pdf
- **Extracted:** 2026-09-19
- **Screening:** Gap-fill for G6 (not in the original 10-record Scopus export)

## Research question
Does liraglutide reduce pain, inflammation, and cartilage catabolism in osteoarthritis models?

## Methods
In vitro chondrocytes/macrophages; in vivo sodium monoiodoacetate OA mouse model with intra-articular liraglutide (Abstract).

## Sample / data
OA (not RA/CIA). Exact mouse n not taken from the abstract extract.

## Key findings
- IA liraglutide alleviated pain-related behaviour in the MIA OA mouse model (Abstract).
- Decreased IL-6, PGE2, and nitric oxide secretion and inflammatory gene expression in vitro, dose-dependent (Abstract).
- Shifted macrophages from M1 toward M2 in vitro; decreased metalloproteinase/aggrecanase activity (Abstract).

## Limitations
OA, not rheumatoid arthritis; intra-articular free liraglutide, not Zhang’s microneedle NP system.

## Relevance
Shows GLP-1 RA biology in a joint disease, but it is a different disease (OA vs Zhang’s CIA/RA) and a different delivery method.
