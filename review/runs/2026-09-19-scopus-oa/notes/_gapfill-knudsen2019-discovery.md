# The Discovery and Development of Liraglutide and Semaglutide

- **Citation:** Knudsen LB, Lau J. Front Endocrinol. 2019;10:155. doi:10.3389/fendo.2019.00155
- **Source file:** papers/scopus-oa-gapfill/Knudsen2019_00155.pdf
- **Extracted:** 2026-09-19
- **Screening:** Gap-fill for G2 (not in the original 10-record Scopus export)

## Research question
How were liraglutide and semaglutide designed (albumin binding) and what human/non-human pharmacology, including oral semaglutide, is described?

## Methods
Narrative discovery/development review by Novo Nordisk authors (title page; Abstract).

## Sample / data
Not a new trial; reviews chemistry, pharmacology, and formulations including oral semaglutide scintigraphy (Fig. 12).

## Key findings
- Authors state oral administration bioavailability was low (~1%) but still produced similar efficacy on glycaemia and weight in the data they review (oral-semaglutide section, ~line discussing “bioavailability was low following oral administration (~1%)”).

## Limitations
Industry authors; narrative review, not a head-to-head PK trial with pulmonary liraglutide.

## Relevance
Independent written statement of oral semaglutide bioavailability in the same order of magnitude as Jeong’s product-literature <2% remark. Still not an oral arm in Jeong’s rats.
