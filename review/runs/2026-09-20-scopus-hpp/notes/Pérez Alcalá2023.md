# Effect of High Hydrostatic Pressure Processing on the Microbiological Quality and Bacterial Diversity of Sous-Vide-Cooked Cod

- **Citation (as given in the paper / filename):** Pérez Alcalá, Diego; Grande Burgos, María José; Rodríguez López, Javier; Lucas, Rosario; Gálvez, Antonio; Pérez Pulido, Rubén. 2023. Foods 12(6): 1206. doi:10.3390/foods12061206. PMC10047930
- **Source file:** papers/2026-09-20-scopus-hpp/extracted/Pérez Alcalá2023.txt
- **Extracted:** 2026-09-20

## Research question
How do high hydrostatic pressure treatments change viable counts and 16S bacterial communities of sous-vide Atlantic cod during long refrigerated storage, and which pressure–temperature–time combination best extends microbiological shelf-life after low-temperature cooking?

## Methods
Atlantic cod (*Gadus morhua*) loins, 25 g, in pil-pil sauce (8 g), vacuum bags. Trial 1: sous-vide 57.1 °C / 30 min, blast chill; half untreated, half HP 500 MPa / 8 min / 22 °C; storage 4 °C up to 60 d; counts on TSA, saline TSA, MacConkey (37 °C, 24–48 h). Trial 2: sous-vide 52 °C / 20 min then HP: A 300 MPa / 8 min / 50 °C; B 600 MPa / 4 min / 50 °C; C 600 MPa / 8 min / 22 °C; D 600 MPa / 8 min / 50 °C; storage up to 105 d. DNA from trial 2 homogenates after PMA treatment; 16S V3–V4 Illumina MiSeq; SILVA 132. Counts: duplicates, plated in triplicate (n = 6 for stats, p < 0.05). Detection limit <1.30 log CFU/g.

## Sample / data
Whole fish ~5.6–5.8 kg, same-day market, scaled, skinned, 25 g portions with emulsified extra-virgin olive oil pil-pil. Two biological replicates pooled per treatment/time for sequencing.

## Claim-ready facts

- **Design:** Two refrigerated storage trials of sous-vide cod ± HP, with culture counts and (trial 2) PMA-16S HTS
- **Population / model:** Sous-vide Atlantic cod loin in pil-pil sauce
- **n:** counts n = 6 (duplicate samples × triplicate plates); diversity from pooled duplicate DNA
- **Intervention / comparator:** Trial 1: 57.1 °C/30 min ± 500 MPa/8 min/22 °C. Trial 2: 52 °C/20 min ± HP A–D (300–600 MPa, 4–8 min, 22 or 50 °C). Refrigeration 4 °C.
- **Primary endpoint:** Total aerobic mesophiles (log CFU/g on TSA) vs time; community composition (relative abundance)
- **Primary result:** Trial 1 controls exceeded 5 log CFU/g by day 45 (5.63 ± 0.06 on TSA); HP stayed <1.30 through day 45 and was 4.92 ± 0.03 at day 60 vs control 7.33 ± 0.03. Trial 2 controls 5.45 log by day 7 and 6.45 by day 14. All HP treatments <1.30 from day 7–30; growth returned from day 45. Authors identify 600 MPa / 4 min / 50 °C as optimal for low-temperature sous-vide. MacConkey counts stayed <1.30 in HP samples. Without HP after 52 °C cooking, shelf-life was <7 days.
- **Cannot show:** Sensory, texture, nutrition, or color of the fish; inactivation kinetics of named pathogens; that Anoxybacillus is a seafood hazard (authors flag it as needing study).

## Key findings
- Trial 1 MacConkey always <1.30 in controls and HP (Table 1).
- Trial 2 controls reached 8.73 (day 30) and 9.59 ± 0.15 (day 45) log CFU/g; plateau 10.5–10.9 log at days 60–90 (Results).
- Controls: Proteobacteria dominate; *Pseudomonas* then Enterobacteriaceae succession; *Anoxybacillus* early, *Carnobacterium* late (Section 3).
- HP late storage: *Aerococcus* and *Enterococcus* predominate depending on HP intensity; *Pseudomonas*/Enterobacteriaceae relative abundances very low vs controls (Section 3).
- *Anoxybacillus flavithermus* reads interpreted as possible kitchen/process contaminant (Discussion).

## Limitations (as stated by the authors, or evident from the methods)
- No previous HTS on HP sous-vide seafood (authors).
- Diversity DNA pooled across two biological replicates.
- Quality attributes (texture/color) not measured despite literature that HP can change seafood proteins.
- Cooking with sauce/oil is a specific matrix, not plain cod.

## Relevance to our research question
Seafood HPP as a hurdle after sous-vide: 500 MPa/8 min after 57.1 °C cooking kept mesophiles undetectable for 45 d; 600 MPa after milder 52 °C cooking delayed growth ≥30 d. Microbiological quality only.

## Open questions / things to verify
- Full trial 2 table after day 45 is truncated in the extract; late HP counts should be taken from the paper tables if used quantitatively.
- “Optimal” 600 MPa/4 min/50 °C is the authors’ ranking, not a sensory optimum.
