# Synergistic effects of ultrasound-assisted ultra-high pressure on quality attributes and flavor profile of lactic acid bacteria-fermented fresh jujube juice

- **Citation (as given in the paper / filename):** Zhang, Yu; Xue, Qinjie; Yao, Yao; Xie, Jiachen; Yan, Jiaxin; Yao, Ruier; Yang, Miao; Sun, Xiaoyan; Yu, Zhihui; Ma, Ling. 2026. Ultrasonics Sonochemistry 130:107903. doi:10.1016/j.ultsonch.2026.107903
- **Source file:** papers/2026-09-20-scopus-hpp/extracted/Zhang2026.txt
- **Extracted:** 2026-09-20

## Research question
Does ultra-high pressure (500 MPa) with or without post-inoculation ultrasound (300 W, 40 kHz), compared with 85 °C pasteurization, improve physicochemical quality, bioactives, colloidal stability, flavor, and sensory scores of mixed LAB-fermented Huping jujube juice over 40 h?

## Methods
Juice: pitted Huping jujube homogenized 1:2 w/v, pectinase 0.8 g/L + cellulase 1.6 g/L at 50 °C for 4 h, enzymes killed 90 °C/10 min, sieved, TSS adjusted with sucrose. Starter: L. plantarum + L. acidophilus 2:1, 1.5% v/v, ≥10^8 CFU/mL, 37 °C anaerobic, 40 h. Five groups: FJJ-C uninoculated; FJJ-P pasteurization 85 °C/15 min then inoculate; FJJ-H UHP 500 MPa/10 min then inoculate; FJJ-P-U pasteurization + US 2 h after inoculum; FJJ-H-U UHP + same US (300 W, 40 kHz, 5 s on/off, 15 min, ≤35 °C). Endpoints: pH, TSS, browning index, CIELab, ascorbic acid, TPC, TFC, reducing sugars, ABTS, DPPH, LAB plate counts, zeta/size/PdI, GC-IMS, HS-SPME-GC–MS, electronic nose, 10-person sensory. Triplicate; ANOVA + Duncan, p < 0.05.

## Sample / data
Fresh Huping jujubes, Taigu, Shanxi, stored at 4 °C. Analyses n = 3. Sensory n = 10 (5 female, 5 male). Sampling 0–40 h (8 h steps for physchem; 0/24/40 h for antioxidants and particle size; 16/40 h LAB; 24 h volatiles).

## Claim-ready facts

- **Design:** Controlled laboratory fermentation study with thermal vs UHP pretreatments ± ultrasound (not a pathogen challenge trial)
- **Population / model:** Enzyme-extracted Huping jujube juice fermented with L. plantarum and L. acidophilus
- **n:** Instrumental assays n = 3; sensory n = 10
- **Intervention / comparator:** UHP 500 MPa/10 min ± US vs pasteurization 85 °C/15 min ± US vs uninoculated juice
- **Primary endpoint:** Authors emphasize combined quality: TPC, ABTS/DPPH, reducing-sugar consumption, particle size, sensory overall acceptability (not a single pathogen log reduction)
- **Primary result:** At 24 h, TPC reached 2.32 mg/mL; ABTS 87.30% and DPPH 74.86% (abstract; DPPH of FJJ-H-U 74.86% and FJJ-P-U 74.97% at 24 h in Section 3.9). Reducing-sugar consumption 54.69% by 40 h in US-UHP (FJJ-H-U 33.47 mg/mL vs ~72.3–74.0 mg/mL at 0 h). Particle size 33.41–53.21 d.nm in treated fermented juices vs 1597.67 d.nm in FJJ-C at 40 h. LAB at 40 h: FJJ-C 1.17 × 10^5 CFU/mL vs other groups >1.0 × 10^8; FJJ-H 4.77 × 10^8 CFU/mL. FJJ-H-U had the highest overall sensory scores; FJJ-C the lowest.
- **Cannot show:** Kill of juice-borne pathogens or a 5-log pasteurization claim (UHP/heat are pretreatments before LAB growth); commercial shelf life after fermentation; that US “inactivates microbes” here (US was used to stimulate LAB at 2 h)

## Key findings
- Terminal pH ~3.4–3.5 in inoculated groups; FJJ-P-U 3.41 and FJJ-H-U 3.40 lowest (Section 3.1). FJJ-H-U TSS minimum 11.12 °Brix at 40 h (Section 3.2).
- Browning index: FJJ-C 0.26 at 40 h vs FJJ-H and FJJ-H-U stable 0.11–0.15 (Section 3.3). FJJ-H L* stayed ~19.2–19.7 late fermentation vs FJJ-C 17.76 → 11.96 (Table 1).
- Ascorbic acid: FJJ-C 0.57 mg/100 g at 40 h; UHP groups higher at 24 and 40 h; FJJ-P-U 0.81 vs FJJ-P 0.44 (Section 3.5; mixed mg/100 g and mg/mL units).
- TFC at 40 h highest in FJJ-H-U 0.16 mg/mL (Section 3.7). ABTS of US groups ~85% at 40 h; DPPH of those groups still >50% vs control (Section 3.9).
- GC-IMS: US-UHP lowered butyric acid, hexanal, acetaldehyde (abstract) and other off-notes vs UHP alone; ketones/esters associated with floral/fruity notes increased. TPC/TFC correlated with overall acceptance r > 0.92 (abstract).

## Limitations (as stated by the authors, or evident from the methods)
- Uninoculated control is not an inoculated-but-unsterilized ferment, so LAB “sterilization benefit” is partly by design.
- Sensory ethics were waived locally; scores are a trained panel of 10, not consumers.
- Ascorbic acid units conflict (mg/100 g vs mg/mL).
- Abstract “microbial inactivation” refers to pretreatment of indigenous flora, while reported LAB counts show growth, not inactivation.
- No refrigerated shelf-life after 40 h fermentation.

## Relevance to our research question
HPP (UHP) + ultrasound as a quality-preserving alternative to 85 °C pasteurization in a juice that is then fermented. Strong on phenolics, color, colloidal stability, and flavor vs heat; weak as a microbial-inactivation primary study.

## Open questions / things to verify

- Pressure come-up/hold temperature during 500 MPa/10 min.
- Whether sucrose standardization confounds TSS as a fermentation-only marker.
