# High Hydrostatic Pressure-Based Combination Strategies for Microbial Inactivation of Food Products: The Cases of Emerging Combination Patterns

- **Citation (as given in the paper / filename):** Xia Q, Liu Q, Denoya GI, Yang C, Barba FJ, Yu H, Chen X. Front. Nutr. 2022, 9:878904. doi: 10.3389/fnut.2022.878904
- **Source file:** papers/2026-09-20-scopus-hpp/Xia2022.pdf
- **Extracted:** 2026-09-20

## Research question
Which non-thermal combinations with high hydrostatic pressure (HHP) improve microbial inactivation in plant foods without relying on HHP-assisted heating?

## Methods
Mini-review of HHP plus emerging non-thermal hurdles (e.g. antimicrobials, other physical fields) in fruit and vegetable matrices; Table 1 compiles combination effects.

## Sample / data
Plant-based foods; cited combination trials. Study n not reported.

## Claim-ready facts
- **Design:** Mini-review of HHP combination strategies.
- **Population / model:** Vegetables and fruits / plant-based foods; pressure-resistant strains and injured-cell recovery are the stated problems for HHP alone.
- **n:** not reported.
- **Intervention / comparator:** Individual HHP versus HHP + non-thermal hurdles; traditional HHP-assisted thermal processing is presented as a nutrition risk.
- **Primary endpoint:** Microbial inactivation efficiency and retention of freshness/phytochemicals.
- **Primary result:** HHP alone cannot assure complete inactivation because of pressure-resistant strains and storage regrowth of injured cells (abstract). One cited combination: 60 min of a hurdle with HHP 450 MPa / 10 min achieved high inactivation in the tabled examples (body; exact paired technology in Table 1).
- **Cannot show:** A ranked best combination for all juices; spore sterilisation by mild HHP combinations.

## Key findings
- Motivation: keep fresh-like V&F while closing the HHP lethality gap without extra heat (Introduction).
- Combinations are framed as process-design problems (integration, not just add-on chemicals).

## Limitations (as stated by the authors, or evident from the methods)
- Mini-review; heterogeneous Table 1 conditions.

## Relevance to our research question
Frames hurdle HPP as the practical answer to incomplete HHP kill in produce.
