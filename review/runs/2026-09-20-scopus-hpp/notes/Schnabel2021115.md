# Plasma-functionalized water: from bench to prototype for fresh-cut lettuce

- **Citation (as given in the paper / filename):** Schnabel, Uta; Handorf, Oliver; Stachowiak, Joerg; Boehm, Daniela; Weit, Christoph; Weihe, Thomas; Schäfer, Jan; Below, Harald; Bourke, Paula; Ehlbeck, Joerg. 2021. Food Engineering Reviews 13:115–135. doi:10.1007/s12393-020-09238-9
- **Source file:** papers/2026-09-20-scopus-hpp/extracted/Schnabel2021115.txt
- **Extracted:** 2026-09-20

## Research question
Can microwave plasma-processed air used to make plasma-treated water (PTW) reduce native microbes on fresh-cut lettuce in a multi-step wash line at laboratory and pilot scale without harming color, texture, or tissue ultrastructure?

## Methods
Atmospheric microwave (2.45 GHz) two-stage source PLexc2 generated plasma-processed air that functionalized distilled water (lab) or tap water (pilot/IBC). Three wash scenarios: (1) tap water only; (2) PTW only in the 180 s main wash; (3) PTW in pre-bath (180 s), pre-rinse (30 s), and main wash (180 s). Native load recovered by stomacher + plate count agar, 30 °C overnight; reduction factor vs unwashed lettuce. Lab only: XTT metabolic activity and LIVE/DEAD BacLight (each repeated threefold with n = 3, total n = 9). Quality: CIELab Chroma and texture analyzer (10 g samples; color/texture repeated 3 days × n = 5 = 15); SEM, AFM, TEM. PTW chemistry: pH; H2O2 by chronoamperometry; nitrite/nitrate by ion chromatography. FTIR of PPA before water contact.

## Sample / data
Organic endive and butterhead lettuce, stored ≤24 h at 4 °C. Initial total plate count 2.5 × 10^6 CFU/g (lab) and 3.1 × 10^7 CFU/g (pilot). Microbial assays n = 9. PTW H2O2 chronoamperometry averaged over five repeats. FTIR: ·NO 2900 ppm, ·NO2 76 ppm, H2O 9200 ppm.

## Claim-ready facts

- **Design:** Laboratory and pilot-scale process-engineering / wash-line experiment (native flora; not a pathogen RCT)
- **Population / model:** Fresh-cut butterhead and endive lettuce with native aerobic flora
- **n:** Microbiology n = 9; XTT and fluorescence n = 9; color/texture n = 15 (3 × 5); H2O2 n = 5
- **Intervention / comparator:** PTW in one or three wash steps vs all-tap-water washing vs unwashed lettuce
- **Primary endpoint:** Reduction factor of native total plate count (log10 CFU/g) at successive wash extraction points
- **Primary result:** Tap water ≈ 1 log10. Lab scenario 2 (PTW in main wash): >3 log10 at step 4, slightly below 3 after post-rinse. Lab scenario 3 (PTW in three steps): 4–5 log10 in PTW steps. Pilot scenario 3: up to ~2 log10. Pilot scenario 2: reductions close to tap water. XTT: scenario 3 metabolic activity ~3% of unwashed; scenario 2 down to ~40% after PTW steps; tap water ~80%. Color Chroma and compression force not significantly different from unwashed.
- **Cannot show:** Validated log reductions of inoculated human pathogens; toxicology or residue compliance for food-contact PTW; that PI staining equals cell death (authors flag VBNC/false-positive PI); industrial recycled-water performance

## Key findings
- PTW pH 1.5–1.7 (lab) and 1.7–1.9 (pilot); H2O2 5.61 mg L−1 (29.39 mM); nitrite 687 mg L−1; nitrate 1227 mg L−1; peroxynitrite not detected (IC) (Chemistry of PTW).
- Antimicrobial effect attributed to NO2−/NO3− plus pH < 2 and low H2O2, not ONOO−.
- Cell-envelope fluorescence was weakly discriminative: tap water already ~60% loss of G/R integrity, so LIVE/DEAD did not track CFU (Fig. 10).
- SEM/AFM: intact stomata after PTW; TEM: organelles similar to unwashed except vacuoles missing (likely prep artifact) (Figs. 12–14).
- Scale-up reduced kill: same scenario dynamics but lower RF at pilot volumes.

## Limitations (as stated by the authors, or evident from the methods)
- Native mixed flora, not standardized inocula.
- Pilot kill lower than lab; recontamination in tap-water steps possible.
- Possible VBNC after plasma-related stress.
- Quality storage limited (color at 0 and 24 h; texture immediately).
- Regulatory/toxicology work still needed (Conclusion).
- Texture n = 5 mixed stem and leaf, so force differences may be underpowered.

## Relevance to our research question
Strong plasma-activated-water evidence on produce: up to 4–5 log native reduction at lab scale with preserved color/texture, driven by acidified nitrite/nitrate chemistry. Pilot scale delivered only about 2 log in the best scenario, which is the practical constraint for wash-water hurdles.
