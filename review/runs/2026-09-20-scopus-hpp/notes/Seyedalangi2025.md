# Combined effects of gliding-arc plasma and C-phycocyanin on antioxidant activity and shelf-life extension of rainbow trout (Oncorhynchus mykiss) fillets

- **Citation (as given in the paper / filename):** Seyedalangi, Maedehsadat; Sari, Amir Hossein; Nowruzi, Bahareh; Anvar, Seyed Amir Ali. 2025. PLOS ONE 20(11): e0336896. doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0336896
- **Source file:** papers/2026-09-20-scopus-hpp/extracted/Seyedalangi2025.txt
- **Extracted:** 2026-09-20

## Research question
Does gliding-arc plasma (GAP; 2 or 5 min) alone or with C-phycocyanin (PCP, 0.065 mg/mL) slow microbial spoilage, lipid/protein oxidation, and sensory decline of rainbow trout fillets stored 18 days at 4 °C, relative to untreated fillets?

## Methods
Factorial refrigerated storage experiment. Six groups: C (untreated), PC-P (PCP dip only), P2-PC and P5-PC (GAP 2 or 5 min, no PCP), P2+PC and P5+PC (PCP then GAP). GAP: Plasmatech-17A, ~10 kV AC, 1.5 kW, 50 Hz, air, 5 cm nozzle-to-surface, room temperature. PCP from Spirulina platensis; fillets 5 × 5 × 1 cm immersed 1 h at 4 °C. Microbiology: TVC and PTC (PCA), coagulase-positive staphylococci (BPA), Salmonella (SCB/XLD), E. coli (MCA), Lactobacillus (MRS), Enterobacteriaceae (VRBG). Chemistry: pH, acidity, PV, TBARS, TVN, TMA, fatty acids (four groups on day 1), Hunter L*a*b*/ΔE. Antioxidants: DPPH, ABTS, FRAP. Sensory: 10 trained panelists, 5-point scale (1 = extremely good, 5 = extremely unacceptable). One-way ANOVA + Tukey, p < 0.05; mean ± SEM.

## Sample / data
Live trout from a Tehran market; 200 fillets total; all laboratory assays in triplicate (n = 3 per cell; SEM reported). Storage days 1, 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18. Fatty-acid profile on C, PC-P, P5-PC, P5+PC (n = 3). Sensory panel n = 10.

## Claim-ready facts

- **Design:** Controlled laboratory storage trial (six treatments × time; not an RCT)
- **Population / model:** Rainbow trout (Oncorhynchus mykiss) fillets, 5 × 5 × 1 cm
- **n:** 200 fillets; chemistry/microbiology n = 3 per treatment × day; sensory n = 10
- **Intervention / comparator:** GAP 2 or 5 min ± PCP 0.065 mg/mL vs untreated control at 4 ± 0.1 °C for 18 days
- **Primary endpoint:** Total viable count (log CFU/g) vs spoilage limit 6 log CFU/g; supporting TVN (mg N/100 g; limit 30)
- **Primary result:** Control TVC 4.39 → 12.08 log CFU/g by day 18, crossing 6 log on day 3 (Table 1). P5+PC 3.50 → 9.23 log CFU/g, crossing 6 log on day 12. Control TVN 8.77 → 53.95 mg N/100 g (over 30 by day 9); P5+PC 8.59 → 29.40, remaining below 30 through day 18 (Table 7). Salmonella and E. coli not detected in any sample.
- **Cannot show:** Pathogen challenge inactivation (no inoculation); industrial-scale plasma; causal mechanism of PCP–plasma synergy beyond observational group differences; human health outcomes

## Key findings
- PTC lowest in P5+PC (2.70 → 7.17 log CFU/g vs control 3.32 → 10.39) (S1 Table / Section 3.2.1).
- Coagulase-positive staphylococci day 18: C 8.36 vs P5+PC 6.70 log CFU/g (Table 2). Enterobacteriaceae day 18: C 10.28 vs P5+PC 7.39 (Table 4). Lactobacillus day 18: C 10.40 vs P5+PC 8.20 log CFU/g (Table 3).
- Day-18 PV: C 0.54 vs P5+PC 0.33 (Table 6). TBARS: C 0.21 → 0.89 vs P5+PC 0.16 → 0.51 (S3 Table). All TBARS remained <2.0 mg MDA/kg.
- DPPH (authors’ units): control 10.91 → 36.41 vs P5+PC 4.20 → 5.23 (Table 10). FRAP: control 20.01 → 11.75 vs P5+PC 35.88 → 25.30 (abstract / S9 Table).
- n-3/n-6 on day 1: C 0.14, P5+PC 0.11 (Table 8). ΔE day 18: C 13.26 vs P5+PC 6.10 (Table 9). Overall sensory acceptability stayed ≥3 until day 12 for P5+PC vs day 3 for control (Fig. 3; Conclusions).

## Limitations (as stated by the authors, or evident from the methods)
- Surface plasma only; fillet interior not treated.
- Hedonic scale is inverted relative to common 1–5 “like” scales; compare only within this paper.
- PCP changes a* and b* (blue pigment); color is not a pure quality marker.
- Discussion text sometimes cites TVC >6 on day 6 for control, while Table 1 already exceeds 6 on day 3; use the table.
- No independent replication of commercial plasma hardware; air as working gas only.

## Relevance to our research question
Cold-plasma (gliding-arc) plus a natural antioxidant hurdle on fish: 5 min GAP + PCP delayed TVC spoilage from 3 to 12 days and kept TVN under 30 mg N/100 g for 18 days, with slower lipid oxidation. Relevant to plasma–quality trade-offs; PCP confounds “plasma-only” color and DPPH interpretation.
