# A Scoping Review of Thermal and Nonthermal Technologies for Listeria monocytogenes Inactivation in Ready-to-Eat Meats

- **Citation (as given in the paper / filename):** Guzel et al. Comprehensive Reviews in Food Science and Food Safety (record year 2026 in Scopus; PMC text 10.1111/1541-4337.70506).
- **Source file:** papers/2026-09-20-scopus-hpp/Guzel2026.txt
- **Extracted:** 2026-09-20

## Research question
What log reductions of L. monocytogenes are reported for thermal and nonthermal technologies on RTE meats, and how do conditions and methods limit comparison?

## Methods
PRISMA-ScR scoping review. PICO: RTE meat, inactivation technologies, untreated controls, log reduction. Scopus, PubMed, Web of Science. Combinations with chemical antimicrobials excluded.

## Sample / data
1111 records → 74 included studies. HPP 29/74 (39.2%); thermal 18/74 (24.2%); ionizing radiation 9; UV 8; pulsed light 4.

## Claim-ready facts
- **Design:** Scoping review (no meta-analysis).
- **Population / model:** L. monocytogenes on RTE meats.
- **n:** 74 studies.
- **Intervention / comparator:** HPP, heat, radiation, UV-C, pulsed light, cold plasma (discussed) vs untreated controls; chemical hurdles excluded at full text.
- **Primary endpoint:** Magnitude of L. monocytogenes log reduction and influence of processing conditions.
- **Primary result:** HPP was the most studied nonthermal method. Reported HPP log reductions ranged from 0 to 7 log. Below 400 MPa, inactivation was typically <1 log even with long holds. At 500–600 MPa, reductions were most commonly 1–7 log CFU/g within ≤5 min. Meta-analysis was not possible: 31/74 (41.9%) lacked SDs; some reported only D-values; 27/74 (36.5%) used a single strain.
- **Cannot show:** A pooled mean log reduction or a technology ranking.

## Key findings
- Fat in the matrix can protect cells during HPP (citing Koutsoumanis et al.).
- Methodological heterogeneity is itself a finding: single-strain challenges and missing variance.

## Limitations (as stated by the authors, or evident from the methods)
- Scoping, not quantitative synthesis; English-only; chemical combinations excluded.

## Relevance to our research question
Best included map of HPP vs other Listeria controls on RTE meat, and it warns against pooling the experimental papers in our sample.
