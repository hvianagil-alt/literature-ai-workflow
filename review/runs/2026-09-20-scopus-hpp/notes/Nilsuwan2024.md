# Quality of Refrigerated Squid Mantle Cut Treated with Mint Extract Subjected to High-Pressure Processing

- **Citation (as given in the paper / filename):** Nilsuwan, Krisana; Palamae, Suriya; Naher, Jasmin; Buamard, Natchaphol; Zhang, Bin; Benjakul, Soottawat. 2024. Foods 13(8): 1264. doi:10.3390/foods13081264. PMC11049107
- **Source file:** papers/2026-09-20-scopus-hpp/extracted/Nilsuwan2024.txt
- **Extracted:** 2026-09-20

## Research question
Do mint extract (ME) and HPP, alone or combined, slow spoilage of refrigerated squid mantle cuts over 15 days, and which combination best holds microbes, TVB/TMA, oxidation, drip/texture, and sensory scores?

## Methods
Completely randomized design, analyses in triplicate, Tukey p < 0.05. ME: 80% ethanol extract of *Mentha arvensis*. Time-kill vs *P. aeruginosa* ATCC 27834 and *Shewanella* spp. TBRC 5775 at 400 mg/L. Planktonic HPP 400 MPa for 0–7 min. Squid (*Loligo vulgaris*; authors also spell *vulguris*): 25.0 ± 0.5 g, 5 × 5 cm mantle cuts, ME 0/200/400 mg/L, vacuum LLDPE-PA, HPP 200 or 400 MPa / 5 min (or 0.1 MPa untreated control). Storage 4 °C, 15 d, every 3 d. TVC, PBC, *Pseudomonas*, H2S-producers, Enterobacteriaceae. TVB/TMA (Conway), pH, PV, TBARS, weight/cooking loss, Warner-Bratzler firmness/toughness. Sensory: 50 untrained panelists, 9-point hedonic (PSU-HREC-2023-007-1-1). NGS (ZymoBIOMICS) on Fresh, CON day 3, M4P4 day 12 (TVC < 10^6 CFU/g).

## Sample / data
Freshly caught squid from Hat Yai market, processed within ~1 h of lab arrival. Treatments coded M0P2, M2P2, M4P2 (200 MPa) and M0P4, M2P4, M4P4 (400 MPa). Acceptable TVC limit cited as 6 log CFU/g.

## Claim-ready facts

- **Design:** Factorial hurdle trial (ME × HPP) with refrigerated storage, plus in vitro kill curves and NGS
- **Population / model:** Squid mantle cuts (*Loligo vulgaris*)
- **n:** triplicate experiments/analyses; sensory n = 50 untrained panelists
- **Intervention / comparator:** ME 0, 200, 400 mg/L then HPP 200 or 400 MPa / 5 min vs untreated CON; 4 °C, 15 d
- **Primary endpoint:** TVC (log CFU/g) vs 6 log limit; TVB (mg N/100 g); sensory overall likeness
- **Primary result:** CON day-0 TVC 2.98 log CFU/g; all 400 MPa treatments ND. CON, M0P2, and M0P4 exceeded the TVC limit by day 6. M4P4 had the lowest TVC at day 12 (6.26 log CFU/g) among remaining samples. Day-0 TVB 2.11–2.35 mg N/100 g all samples; day 6 CON 25.87; day 12 M4P4 15.21 vs M2P2 20.00, M4P2 21.23, M2P4 18.00. Day-15 M4P4 TMA 7.18 mg N/100 g (lowest); pH 7.45 (lowest); PV 8.01 vs CON 21.25 mg cumene hydroperoxide/kg; TBARS M4P4 0.42 mg MDA eq/kg. Day-0 sensory CON vs M4P4 not different; day 12 M4P4 overall likeness 6.45 (CON not tasted; TVC over limit). NGS: *Brochothrix campestris* (Listeriaceae) predominated in M4P4 at day 12.
- **Cannot show:** Thermal comparator; commercial pack sizes; vitamin nutrition; that HPP alone (without ME) meets 15-day TVC limits.

## Key findings
- ME 400 mg/L in broth: 24 h counts 2.10 (*P. aeruginosa*) and 2.34 (*Shewanella*) log CFU/mL, reductions 2.67 and 2.58 log (Section 3.1).
- HPP 400 MPa, 5 min: planktonic counts 1.88 and 2.09 log CFU/mL; 7 min 1.75 and 2.02 (not different from 5 min) (Section 3.2).
- CON PBC 6.58 log CFU/g at day 6; CON PDC 6.18 at day 6 (Section 3.3.1).
- Weight loss at 15 d: M4P4 7.18%; after 3 d all samples 2.45–2.57% (Section 3.3.5).
- Authors recommend ME 400 mg/L + HPP 400 MPa as combined hurdles (Conclusions).

## Limitations (as stated by the authors, or evident from the methods)
- HPP damage was reversible: TVC rose after day 3 even in 400 MPa samples (Section 3.3.1).
- Latin species spelling inconsistent (*vulgaris* vs *vulguris*).
- Sensory only CON vs M4P4; CON omitted at day 12 for safety.
- No heat-processed squid arm.

## Relevance to our research question
Seafood hurdle study: mint extract plus 400 MPa/5 min slows TVC, TVB/TMA, oxidation, and drip versus untreated squid and versus HPP without extract. Quality and shelf-life focused; nutrition not measured.

## Open questions / things to verify
- Day-12 M4P4 TVC 6.26 log is at/above the 6 log limit they cite, yet they still ran sensory and NGS on the “TVC lower than 6 log” rule—borderline; treat 12 d as the practical end, not a clear pass.
- Texture numeric tables (firmness N) are in figures/tables not fully extracted.
