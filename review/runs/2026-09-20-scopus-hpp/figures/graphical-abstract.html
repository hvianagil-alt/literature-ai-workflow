High-pressure processing when heat is not the kill step

A set-point is not a pasteurisation number — log, law, and quality are separate readings of the same cycle.

1. Condition

Packed food under isostatic 300–600 MPa, seconds to minutes, usually chilled or ambient. Piece size does not create a cold centre.

 →

2. Mechanism (graded)

Reviews compile membrane leak, lipid-phase change, and non-covalent protein unfolding. Ice and lactate change the mechanical or solute coefficients. Not one proven molecular cause for every log.

 →

3. What is measured

Colonies, enzyme activity, or texture. Selective agar and immediate sampling can miss injury. No colony is not automatically death.

 →

4. Three readings

Hurdle: refrigerated vegetative kill at 500–600 MPa in high-aw foods.
Law: industrial milk HPP is not legal heat pasteurisation on the included comparison; juice APC is not a named-pathogen 5-log.
Spores: ambient commercial HPP is not a botulinum cook — HPTP or a cold chain.

Quality (polyphenol oxidase, bioactive proteins, kelp hardness) can move against plate counts on the same cycle. Ultrasound, plasma, and irradiation share a label with pressure, not a mechanism. Magnitudes stay in Tables 1–3 of the article; this panel does not pool them.
