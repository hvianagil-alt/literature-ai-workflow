# Synthesis rationale — 2026-09-19-nanocarriers

## (a) What each included study actually measured

- Lutta 2025: microfluidic pectin coating of CAF04 liposomes; size/PDI/zeta/AFM; n=3 characterization. No in vivo.
- Muenraya 2022: Col-AgNP synthesis; MIC/MBC vs three Gram-negatives; hemolysis/renal cells.
- Heidarli 2017: narrative map of stimulus-responsive cancer liposomes (pre-2018).
- Valkova 2025: 10 nm AgNPs + vancomycin; FIC 0.25 vs VRE; fluorescence microscopy mechanism.
- Mahmoudzadeh 2021: all-atom MD of DOPE–CHMS ± PEG; cites prior calcein % at pH 5.5.
- Felding 2026: AINI DoE of Otrivin deposition (angle, flow, temperature). Not a nanocarrier.
- Chauhan 2020 (AJP OA): anionic sertraline liposomes; size 154 nm; EE 69.46%; rat CUMS behavior. Not JDDST PK.
- Lee 2017: WIREs review, literature window 2008–2016.
- Torres 2025: endogenous/dual-responsive liposomes review.
- Alrbyawi 2024: 5-FU stimuli-responsive liposomes in CRC, review.
- Cheng 2025: liposomes vs LNPs; clinical translation of active targeting is hard.
- Pasarin 2023: polymer coatings (pectin, chitosan, alginate) for liposome stability, review.
- Ferreira 2021: antibiotic liposomes and approved lipid products, review.
- Rustad 2022: PEG750 vs PEG2000 on DOPE:CHEMS calcein release and GL261 uptake.
- Nunes 2022: Lip-DOX vs Lip2000-DOX in 4T1 mice, n=7/group; 60.4% tumor-growth inhibition without PEG.
- Azimzadeh 2025: 10 CRPA isolates; colistin+green AgNPs; biofilm/QS.
- Dove 2023: AgNPs + aminoglycosides; ~22-fold amikacin MIC drop; host-toxicity assays.
- Aloss 2023 (gap-fill): liposomal DOX review including HEAT LTLD phase III miss on PFS.
- Guo 2023 (gap-fill): lung biofilm nanocarriers; ~1000-fold biofilm tolerance; not CRS polymersomes.

Did not measure: Andresen 2026 polymersome full dataset (PDF not retrieved).

## (b) Themes the data support vs themes that would be forced

Supported: (1) a release trigger is the design answer to EPR-without-release; (2) PEG stabilizes circulation-facing membranes and can blunt pH triggers; (3) polymer coats (pectin) change size and charge for mucosal aims; (4) AgNPs can lower antibiotic MICs in vitro, including FIC 0.25 for vancomycin–VRE; (5) nasal deposition depends on angle/temperature/flow even for a simple spray; (6) a PEGylated thermosensitive DOX liposome can fail a phase III PFS endpoint.

Forced if claimed: unified clinical efficacy of “smart liposomes”; that Col-AgNPs beat free colistin on MIC (table shows free colistin 1 µg/mL); that AINI Otrivin results equal liposome sprays; that Andresen’s polymersomes work (unread).

## (c) Real disagreements and why

- PEG: Mahmoudzadeh/Rustad/Nunes agree PEG can reduce pH-trigger or uptake; Aloss’s LTLD still uses DSPE-PEG2000 because circulation and heat-trigger are a different lipid design (lyso-lipid TSL vs DOPE/CHEMS).
- Colistin–AgNP: Muenraya emphasizes conjugate vs AgNPs; Azimzadeh emphasizes checkerboard on CRPA biofilms. Different particles (chemical conjugate vs green AgNPs) and endpoints (planktonic MIC vs biofilm/QS).
- “Higher activity than colistin” (Muenraya Abstract) vs free colistin MIC 1 µg/mL: incommensurable phrasing vs table.

## (d) What this sample cannot answer

- Human efficacy of pectin-coated CAF04 or sertraline liposomes.
- Head-to-head of polymersomes vs liposomes in CRS (Andresen PDF missing).
- Whether AgNP–antibiotic synergy survives inhalation/nasal mucus at safe Ag doses.
- Pooled meta-analytic effect of “stimuli-responsive liposomes.”
