# Silver nanoparticles enhance the efficacy of aminoglycosides against antibiotic-resistant bacteria

- **Citation (as given in the paper / filename):** Dove AS, Dzurny DI, et al. Front Microbiol. 2022/2023. doi:10.3389/fmicb.2022.1064095
- **Source file:** papers/2026-09-19-nanocarriers-found/Dove2023_064095.pdf
- **Extracted:** 2026-09-19
- **Screening:** Included (AgNP adjuvant for aminoglycosides; CDC AR Bank strains)

## Research question
Can AgNPs act as adjuvants to aminoglycosides against MDR bacteria, and are they cytotoxic to host cells or C. elegans?

## Methods
AgNP MIC/MBC; checkerboard with amikacin; CDC/FDA AR Bank strains; C. elegans lifespan; mammalian cytotoxicity (Abstract; Methods).

## Sample / data
E. coli, P. aeruginosa, S. aureus, A. baumannii, Salmonella, K. pneumoniae, C. freundii (Methods).

## Claim-ready facts
- **Design:** in vitro microbiology + limited host-toxicity assays
- **Population / model:** MDR clinical/AR Bank bacteria; C. elegans; mammalian cells
- **n:** MIC experiments described as three independent runs (Methods)
- **Intervention / comparator:** AgNPs ± aminoglycosides (amikacin emphasized)
- **Primary endpoint:** MIC, FICI, MBC/MIC; host toxicity
- **Primary result:** ~22-fold reduction in amikacin MIC with AgNPs (Abstract). AgNP MIC in E. coli DH5α ~3 µg/mL; MIC 3.1–3.125 µg/mL and MBC/MIC = 2 in the reported E. coli experiment (Results). Authors report no cytotoxicity, no C. elegans lifespan effect, no proteotoxicity in the assays used (Abstract).
- **Cannot show:** colistin or vancomycin specifically (those are other papers)

## Key findings
- Positions AgNPs as antibiotic adjuvants rather than stand-alone drugs (Abstract).

## Limitations (as stated by the authors, or evident from the methods)
- Mechanisms of synergy still partly open (Introduction).

## Relevance to our research question
AgNP–antibiotic synergy class that includes aminoglycosides (tobramycin-relevant for the unrecovered polymersome paper).
