## Research question
How have physical and biological stimuli-responsive liposomes been used to deliver 5-fluorouracil to colorectal cancer while addressing short half-life and low bioavailability?

## Methods
Narrative review of 5-FU liposomal platforms responding to magnetic field, light, temperature, and endogenous cues (Abstract).

## Sample / data
Secondary literature. Mentions historical 5-FU survival ~10.8–11.3 months in cited chemotherapy (Introduction).
