# Formulation and Evaluation of Negatively Charged Liposomes of Sertraline Hydrochloride for Antidepressant Activity

- **Citation (as given in the paper / filename):** Chauhan T et al. Asian J Pharm. 2020;14(2):211–. doi:10.22377/ajp.v14i2.3616. OA substitute for the paywalled JDDST 2020 paper of similar title.
- **Source file:** papers/2026-09-19-nanocarriers/Chauhan2020_sertraline.pdf
- **Extracted:** 2026-09-19
- **Screening:** Included (anionic sertraline liposomes; note venue is AJP not JDDST)

## Research question
Can negatively charged sertraline HCl liposomes be formulated with controlled release and antidepressant-like activity in a chronic unpredictable mild stress rat model versus a marketed tablet?

## Methods
Film hydration (HSPC/cholesterol/DMPG as in Methods); size, entrapment, release kinetics; CUMS behavioral tests at 5 mg/kg (Methods).

## Sample / data
Female Wistar rats 200 ± 20 g, 6–8 weeks (Methods). Dose 5 mg/kg.

## Claim-ready facts
- **Design:** formulation + in vitro release + rat behavioral pharmacology
- **Population / model:** female Wistar rats, CUMS
- **n:** rat N not stated as a number in the extracted Methods beyond housing conditions
- **Intervention / comparator:** anionic sertraline liposomes vs marketed sertraline HCl
- **Primary endpoint:** size, %entrapment, release model; swimming/immobility/struggling
- **Primary result:** mean size 154.10 nm; entrapment 69.46% ± 1; Higuchi release; authors report increased swimming and immobility time and decreased struggling vs marketed formulation (Abstract; Results).
- **Cannot show:** human PK; the JDDST pharmacokinetic tables were not retrieved

## Key findings
- Controlled release over the sampled 24 h window (Abstract).

## Limitations (as stated by the authors, or evident from the methods)
- Behavioral endpoints only in this OA paper; “increased immobility” is an unusual direction for an antidepressant assay and should be read against the paper’s scoring definitions.
- Small extracted methods section does not give group n.

## Relevance to our research question
Anionic liposome case outside cancer/infection; charge as a design variable.

## Open questions / things to verify
Confirm whether immobility increase is a reporting inconsistency.
