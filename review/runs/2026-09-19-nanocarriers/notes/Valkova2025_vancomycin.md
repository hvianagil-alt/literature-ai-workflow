# Revealing the mechanism of synergistic antibacterial effect of silver nanoparticles in combination with vancomycin against Enterococcus species by fluorescence microscopy visualization

- **Citation (as given in the paper / filename):** Válková L, Suchánková Hochvaldová L, Mistrík M, Kolář M, Langová K, Kolářová H, Štefková B, Prucek R, Kvítek L, Panáček A. J Mater Chem B. 2025. doi:10.1039/d5tb01231g
- **Source file:** papers/2026-09-19-nanocarriers/Valkova2025_vancomycin.pdf
- **Extracted:** 2026-09-19
- **Screening:** Included (AgNP + vancomycin vs Enterococcus / VRE)

## Research question
What cellular mechanism explains synergy between ~10 nm AgNPs and vancomycin against vancomycin-susceptible E. faecalis and vancomycin-resistant E. faecium?

## Methods
Rhodamine-labelled 10 nm AgNPs; fluorescent vancomycin conjugate; MIC/FIC; confocal/SIM fluorescence microscopy (Abstract; Methods).

## Sample / data
E. faecalis and VRE E. faecium; Table 1 MIC/FIC.

## Claim-ready facts
- **Design:** in vitro microbiology + high-resolution fluorescence microscopy
- **Population / model:** vancomycin-susceptible E. faecalis and vancomycin-resistant E. faecium
- **n:** not stated as independent biological n in the extracted Table 1 caption
- **Intervention / comparator:** AgNPs or Ag-RBITC ± vancomycin vs each agent alone
- **Primary endpoint:** MIC (mg L−1) and FIC
- **Primary result:** vs VRE, AgNPs/Ag-RBITC MIC 13.5 mg L−1 alone; vancomycin 512 mg L−1 alone; combination Ag 3.38 mg L−1 + vancomycin 2 mg L−1, FIC 0.25 (Table 1). Mechanism: oxidative-stress-related cell-wall failure plus intracellular AgNP DNA deformation (Abstract).
- **Cannot show:** in vivo PK/PD or liposomal co-formulation

## Key findings
- Synergy classified at FIC 0.25 against VRE (Table 1).
- Multi-level non-specific mechanism (Abstract).

## Limitations (as stated by the authors, or evident from the methods)
- In vitro only; labelling may alter particle behavior (authors use labelled particles for imaging).

## Relevance to our research question
Gram-positive counterpart to colistin–AgNP work; mechanism of restoring a cell-wall antibiotic.
