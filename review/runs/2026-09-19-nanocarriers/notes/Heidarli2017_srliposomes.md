# State of the Art of Stimuli-Responsive Liposomes for Cancer Therapy

- **Citation (as given in the paper / filename):** Heidarli E, Dadashzadeh S, Haeri A. Iran J Pharm Res. 2017;16(4):1273-1304. PMID:29552041 PMCID:PMC5843293

## Research question
How do external (heat, magnetic, light, ultrasound) and internal (pH, reduction, enzyme) stimuli-responsive liposomes address poor intra-tumoral release and endosomal trapping of long-circulating liposomes?

## Methods
Narrative review of published liposome designs (no new experiment).

## Sample / data
Literature corpus through ~2017 (not a systematic PRISMA count).

- **Population / model:** not applicable (secondary literature on cancer nanocarriers)
