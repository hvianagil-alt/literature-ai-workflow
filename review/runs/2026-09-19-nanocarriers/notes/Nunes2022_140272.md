# PEGylated versus Non-PEGylated pH-Sensitive Liposomes: New Insights from a Comparative Antitumor Activity Study

- **Citation (as given in the paper / filename):** Nunes SS, de Souza ÂC, et al. Pharmaceutics. 2022;14:272. doi:10.3390/pharmaceutics14020272
- **Source file:** papers/2026-09-19-nanocarriers-found/Nunes2022_140272.pdf
- **Extracted:** 2026-09-19
- **Screening:** Included (DOX pH-sensitive liposomes ± PEG in 4T1 mice)

## Research question
Does PEGylation help or hurt antitumor activity of doxorubicin-loaded pH-sensitive liposomes in 4T1-bearing mice?

## Methods
Lip-DOX vs Lip2000-DOX (DSPE-PEG2000); DLS/Cryo-TEM; 4T1 uptake; BALB/c 4T1 model, n=7/group, five arms including blanks and free DOX (Methods).

## Sample / data
4T1 cells; BALB/c mice n=7 per group (Methods).

## Claim-ready facts
- **Design:** formulation + in vitro uptake + mouse efficacy
- **Population / model:** 4T1 murine breast tumors in BALB/c mice
- **n:** n=7 per treatment group (Methods)
- **Intervention / comparator:** Lip-DOX vs Lip2000-DOX vs free DOX vs blank liposomes
- **Primary endpoint:** tumor growth inhibition; lung metastases histology; size/PDI/zeta/EE
- **Primary result:** Size ~140 nm, PDI <0.2, EE >90% (Abstract; Lip-DOX 139.4 ± 3.8 nm, EE 93.1 ± 1.2%, Table). Lip-DOX had better cellular uptake than Lip2000-DOX. Lip-DOX inhibited tumor growth by 60.4%; no metastatic foci in Lip-DOX lungs (Abstract).
- **Cannot show:** human breast-cancer outcomes or circulation half-life tables in this extract

## Key findings
- Authors conclude pH-sensitive liposomes may work well without PEG (Abstract).

## Limitations (as stated by the authors, or evident from the methods)
- Immunocompetent mouse 4T1 model only.

## Relevance to our research question
In vivo test of the PEG penalty on a pH-sensitive DOX liposome.
