# Microfluidic fabrication of pectin-coated liposomes for drug delivery

- **Citation (as given in the paper / filename):** Lutta A, Liu Q, Pedersen GK, Dong M, Grohganz H, Nielsen LH, et al. Drug Deliv Transl Res. 2025;15:2941-2950. doi:10.1007/s13346-025-01812-0
- **Source file:** papers/2026-09-19-nanocarriers/Lutta2025_pectin.pdf
- **Extracted:** 2026-09-19
- **Screening:** Included (pectin-coated CAF04 liposomes; microfluidic process)

## Research question
Can microfluidics reproducibly coat cationic CAF04 liposomes with structurally different pectins, and which pectin/liposome ratio and flow-rate ratio give a stable coating?

## Methods
CAF04 (DDA:MMG) liposomes coated with LM, LMA, or HM pectin on a Dolomite laminar-flow microfluidic chip. FRR 2:1–5:1 pectin:liposome; DLS size/PDI/zeta; AFM; storage stability (Results; Methods).

## Sample / data
Formulation study, n=3 ± SD for Table 2 characterization (Table 2). No in vivo arm in this paper.

## Claim-ready facts
- **Design:** in vitro formulation / process-optimization study
- **Population / model:** CAF04 cationic liposomes; LM, LMA, HM pectins
- **n:** n=3 technical replicates for size/PDI/zeta (Table 2)
- **Intervention / comparator:** uncoated liposomes vs pectin-coated at FRR 2:1 (pectin/liposome weight ratio 0.7)
- **Primary endpoint:** hydrodynamic size, PDI, zeta potential
- **Primary result:** uncoated ~220 ± 12 nm; LM-pectin 965 ± 178 nm, zeta −38 ± 1.74 mV; LMA 1211 ± 106 nm, −40 ± 3.17 mV; HM 1098 ± 140 nm, −18 ± 3.68 mV (Table 2). Optimal pectin/liposome weight ratio 0.7 and FRR 2:1 (Abstract).
- **Cannot show:** oral protection, mucin interaction, or in vivo delivery (those are cited as follow-on work, not measured here)

## Key findings
- Coating increased size at least threefold and shifted zeta from cationic to strongly negative, interpreted as full coating (Abstract; Table 2).
- AFM: pectins formed a polymer network with liposomes dispersed or attached (Abstract).
- Coatings remained on storage (Abstract).

## Limitations (as stated by the authors, or evident from the methods)
- Technical note; no drug-release or animal data.
- TFR text is poorly parsed (“3–6 mL”) — treat flow magnitudes with caution.

## Relevance to our research question
Direct methods evidence for polymer-coated liposomes aimed at mucosal/oral use.
