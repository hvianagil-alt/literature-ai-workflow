# Mechanistic Insight into How PEGylation Reduces the Efficacy of pH-Sensitive Liposomes from Molecular Dynamics Simulations

- **Citation (as given in the paper / filename):** Mahmoudzadeh M, Magarkar A, Koivuniemi A, Róg T, Bunker A. Mol Pharm. 2021;18(7):2612-2621. doi:10.1021/acs.molpharmaceut.1c00122
- **Source file:** papers/2026-09-19-nanocarriers/Mahmoudzadeh2021_pegmd.txt
- **Extracted:** 2026-09-19
- **Screening:** Included (MD of PEGylated DOPE–CHMS pH-sensitive bilayers). PDF blocked at PMC; ACS HTML full text used.

## Research question
Why does PEG reduce pH-triggered release from DOPE–CHMS liposomes, how does CHSa stabilize the lamellar phase at pH 7.4, and how does protonation to CHS destabilize it?

## Methods
All-atom MD of DOPE–CHMS bilayers with/without PEG (Abstract). Experimental calcein-release numbers are cited from prior literature, not newly measured here.

## Sample / data
Simulation systems (not a clinical n). Cited experimental release: DOPE–CHMS 55% calcein at pH 5.5 vs 24% with 0.6 mol% DSPE-PEG2000 and 10% with 3 mol% (Introduction, citing ref 35).

## Claim-ready facts
- **Design:** all-atom molecular dynamics; mechanistic
- **Population / model:** DOPE bilayers with CHSa/CHS ± PEG
- **n:** not applicable (simulation)
- **Intervention / comparator:** CHSa vs CHS; PEGylated vs non-PEGylated DOPE bilayers
- **Primary endpoint:** area per lipid, hydrophilicity, PEG penetration / H-bonds
- **Primary result:** CHSa increases surface hydrophilicity (stabilizes lamellar); CHS reduces hydrophilicity and area per lipid (favors destabilization); PEG increases area per lipid by penetrating the DOPE bilayer, stabilizing lamellar phase and thereby reducing pH sensitivity (Abstract; Conclusions).
- **Cannot show:** in vivo circulation or tumor efficacy

## Key findings
- PEG–DOPE H-bonds are argued to exceed PEG–PC behavior because PC lacks H-bond donors (Discussion).

## Limitations (as stated by the authors, or evident from the methods)
- Simulation of bilayers, not intact liposomes in blood.
- Calcein % values are from cited experiments.

## Relevance to our research question
Mechanistic core of the PEG vs pH-trigger trade-off.
