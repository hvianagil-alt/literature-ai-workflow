# Biofilm inhibition of multidrug-resistant Pseudomonas aeruginosa using green-synthesized silver nanoparticles and colistin

- **Citation (as given in the paper / filename):** Azimzadeh M, Greco G, Farmani A, et al. Sci Rep. 2025;15:14993. doi:10.1038/s41598-025-00005-6
- **Source file:** papers/2026-09-19-nanocarriers-found/Azimzadeh2025_00056.pdf
- **Extracted:** 2026-09-19
- **Screening:** Included (colistin + green AgNPs vs CRPA biofilms)

## Research question
Do green-synthesized AgNPs plus colistin synergize against clinical carbapenem-resistant P. aeruginosa biofilms and quorum-sensing gene expression?

## Methods
10 burn-wound P. aeruginosa isolates; checkerboard FIC; MBIC/FBIC; SEM; RT-PCR of QS genes; L929 cytotoxicity (Abstract; Methods).

## Sample / data
10 clinical isolates + ATCC 27853; all strong biofilm formers (Abstract).

## Claim-ready facts
- **Design:** in vitro clinical-isolate microbiology + gene expression
- **Population / model:** MDR/CRPA burn isolates; murine L929 fibroblasts for IC50
- **n:** 10 clinical isolates
- **Intervention / comparator:** AgNPs, colistin, AgNPs@CL
- **Primary endpoint:** MIC, FIC (≤0.5 synergy), MBIC; L929 viability
- **Primary result:** Colistin MIC 8–128 µg/mL; AgNP MIC 0.07–37.5 µg/mL (Results). All isolates synergistic or additive for AgNPs@CL (Abstract). L929 IC50 75 µg/mL AgNPs; >50% viability at 37.5 µg/mL (Results).
- **Cannot show:** in vivo pneumonia/sinus models

## Key findings
- QS-regulated genes decreased in ATCC 27853 and isolate #354 under synergistic conditions (Abstract).

## Limitations (as stated by the authors, or evident from the methods)
- Small isolate panel from one region; green synthesis may not match chemically defined Col-AgNPs (Muenraya).

## Relevance to our research question
Clinical-isolate extension of colistin–AgNP combinations into biofilms.
