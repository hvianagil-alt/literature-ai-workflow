# Recent Preclinical and Clinical Progress in Liposomal Doxorubicin

- **Citation (as given in the paper / filename):** Aloss K, Hamar P. Pharmaceutics. 2023;15:893. doi:10.3390/pharmaceutics15030893
- **Source file:** papers/2026-09-19-nanocarriers-gapfill/Makwana2023_030893.pdf
- **Extracted:** 2026-09-19
- **Screening:** Gap-fill (clinical thermosensitive doxorubicin / LTLD)

## Research question
What preclinical and clinical progress has been made with liposomal doxorubicin, including thermosensitive (LTLD/ThermoDox) combinations with hyperthermia or radiofrequency ablation?

## Methods
Narrative review of liposomal doxorubicin products and trials (Abstract).

## Sample / data
Secondary literature including HEAT (NCT00617981).

## Claim-ready facts
- **Design:** narrative review of liposomal doxorubicin
- **Population / model:** hepatocellular carcinoma and other solid-tumor trial literature
- **n:** phase I LTLD plus ablation, n=24 hepatocellular carcinoma (body)
- **Intervention / comparator:** LTLD (DPPC:MSPC:DSPE-PEG2000 86:10:4) plus radiofrequency ablation versus ablation alone
- **Primary endpoint:** HEAT primary progression-free survival; overall survival also reported
- **Primary result:** HEAT did not meet its primary progression-free survival endpoint; progression-free and overall survival were similar for LTLD 50 mg/m2 given 15 min before ablation versus ablation alone in 3–7 cm hepatocellular carcinoma. A subgroup had better overall survival when ablation lasted at least 45 minutes (LTLD section).
- **Cannot show:** a new patient-level re-analysis

## Key findings
- LTLD is described as the first thermosensitive liposome to enter human trials (body).

## Limitations (as stated by the authors, or evident from the methods)
- Review of published trials; the 45-minute ablation finding is a subgroup analysis as presented.
