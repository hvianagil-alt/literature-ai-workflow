# Silver Nanoparticles Conjugated with Colistin Enhanced the Antimicrobial Activity against Gram-Negative Bacteria

- **Citation (as given in the paper / filename):** Muenraya P, Sawatdee S, Srichana T, Atipairin A. Molecules. 2022;27:5780. doi:10.3390/molecules27185780
- **Source file:** papers/2026-09-19-nanocarriers/Muenraya2022_colistin.pdf
- **Extracted:** 2026-09-19
- **Screening:** Included (colistin–AgNP conjugate)

## Research question
Does conjugating colistin to AgNPs improve Gram-negative antibacterial activity and cell compatibility versus free colistin or AgNPs alone?

## Methods
Chemical synthesis of Col-AgNPs (formulations F0–F5); FT-IR, EDX, TEM, DLS, PXRD; broth MIC/MBC vs E. coli, K. pneumoniae, P. aeruginosa; hemolysis and renal-cell viability (Abstract; Methods).

## Sample / data
Indicator strains TISTR collection; cytotoxicity on human RBCs and renal cells (Abstract).

## Claim-ready facts
- **Design:** in vitro formulation + microbiology + cytotoxicity
- **Population / model:** E. coli TISTR 887, K. pneumoniae, P. aeruginosa; human RBCs and renal cells
- **n:** not reported as biological replicate n in the Abstract; characterization means ± SD (Table 1)
- **Intervention / comparator:** Col-AgNPs (optimized F3) vs AgNPs (F0) vs free colistin
- **Primary endpoint:** MIC/MBC (µg/mL); drug loading %; hemolysis
- **Primary result:** F3 drug loading 11.55 ± 0.93%; Col-AgNP MIC/MBC in the 4.0–16.0 µg/mL range vs the three Gram-negatives, with F3 at 4.0 µg/mL highlighted; free colistin MIC/MBC 1 µg/mL; F0 AgNPs 64 and 128 µg/mL bacteriostatic/bactericidal (Results; Table 2). Compatible up to 16 µg/mL on RBCs and renal cells; at 32 µg/mL Col-AgNPs had higher cell survival than AgNPs or colistin (Abstract).
- **Cannot show:** in vivo nephroprotection or clinical PK

## Key findings
- Mechanism attributed to membrane disruption and genomic DNA damage (Abstract).
- F3 zeta −30.93 ± 0.85 mV at t0 (Table 1).

## Limitations (as stated by the authors, or evident from the methods)
- Laboratory strains, not a clinical trial.
- Free colistin still had a lower MIC (1 µg/mL) than Col-AgNPs; benefit is framed as activity vs AgNPs plus improved compatibility at 32 µg/mL.

## Relevance to our research question
Seed for antibiotic–AgNP combination against Gram-negatives, including P. aeruginosa.

## Open questions / things to verify
Whether “higher activity than colistin” in the Abstract is vs AgNPs+colistin separately or strictly vs free colistin MIC (Table 2 shows free colistin MIC 1 µg/mL).
