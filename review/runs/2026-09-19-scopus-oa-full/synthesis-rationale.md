# Synthesis rationale — 2026-09-19-scopus-oa-full

**Research question:** In this Scopus open-access export (2024–2026), what is reported about GLP-1 receptor agonists, stem-cell/exosome products, and drug-delivery systems in metabolic disease (diabetes, obesity, prediabetes, and closely related inflammatory or repair settings)?

**Included from the export:** 44 full texts (PRISMA: 459 identified; 274 title exclude; 185 sought; 139 not retrieved; 46 assessed; 2 full-text exclude).

**Gap-fill (not in the export):** Pechenov 2021 (*Sci Rep*; MEDI7219 oral GLP-1 RA in dogs), retrieved to contextualize oral peptide bioavailability.

**Not used as evidence:** 139 titles without a public PDF; Qiao 2025 (IBD delivery; GLP-1 only in passing); Qiu 2026 (downloaded file was a different *JCI* article).

This file is the interpretation step. It is not a token or phase meter.

## (a) What each included study actually measured

**Human trials and protocols.** Pasqua 2025: double-blind crossover weekly SC semaglutide vs placebo as adjunct to automated insulin delivery in adults with T1D (28 randomized, 24 completers); primary endpoint time in range. Chen 2024: retrospective T2D time-in-range after GLP-1 RA-based vs other oral therapy (202 propensity-matched). Nielsen 2025: bioequivalence of two oral semaglutide tablet generations in healthy participants. Ma 2024: food-effect PK of oral orforglipron (fed vs fasted AUC/Cmax). Hirotsu 2025: small Japanese pilot of oral semaglutide after DPP-4 inhibitors. Fragozo-Ramos 2025 and Tian (neurology) 2025: **protocols** (AID-JUNCT; taVNS + semaglutide GI effects)—no outcome data. Lunati 2026: two-patient LADY case report (oral semaglutide + basal insulin).

**Safety, utilisation, simulation, infodemiology.** Sillassen 2025: SR/MA of 50 placebo-controlled semaglutide trials at raised CV risk (54,972 randomized); mortality, MI, SAEs, GI AEs. Xiong 2024: FAERS oral semaglutide tablets 2019–2023. Yue 2025: FAERS plus preclinical retinal endothelium for semaglutide and diabetic retinopathy. Shen 2025: Mendelian randomization + SR of GLP-1 RAs and DR in European-ancestry data. Swift 2024: real-world lines of therapy after starting oral semaglutide. Wang 2025: Medicare disparities in SGLT2i/GLP-1 RA uptake 2013–2019. Abdallah 2025: GLP-1 RAs after bariatric IWL/regain. Ali 2025: LMIC microsimulation of SGLT2i + GLP-1 RA introduction. Raubenheimer 2024: Google Trends for semaglutide interest during shortages.

**Narrative reviews.** Bonga 2025, Son 2026: incretin analogues for obesity. Wallentowitz 2026: incretins in maternal obesity/metabolic syndrome. Zachou 2024: anti-diabetic drugs in NAFLD. Hölscher 2025: GLP-1/GIP in AD/PD. Singh 2026: diabetes therapy + PLGA insulin microspheres (review).

**Non-injectable / local delivery (preclinical unless noted).** Jeong 2026: pulmonary liraglutide–ZnO; relative BA ~60%/51% vs SC in rats. Woo 2025: HA/aminoclay microneedle semaglutide in rats. Yao 2026: adipose-targeted MN co-delivering semaglutide + rosiglitazone. Zhang 2026: MN + silk NP “GLP-1RAs” in CIA rats (EE ~28–33%; ~60% release by day 14). Domingues 2024: oral semaglutide lipid nanocapsules in MASLD models. Sandmeier 2025: SEDDS reverse micelles vs HIP for semaglutide. Kweon 2024: liraglutide nanoformulation (ASBT/EGFR). Dangi 2025: modified-release semaglutide (FPR-8). Klotsman 2024: 84-day exenatide implant in five cats. Fredholt 2026: C10 mini-tablet vs liquid **insulin** in rats. Yu 2025: acetylated long-acting **insulin** vs icodec. Xu 2026: biosynthetic tirzepatide variants in *E. coli*.

**Extra-glycaemic / mechanism.** da Silva Lima 2024: no direct GLP-1/GIP action on human hepatocytes/HSCs at insulin-secretory concentrations. Mastaitis 2025: GDF8/activin A blockade spares muscle during GLP-1 treatment (mice, NHP). Abo-Elenin 2025 and Tian (pharmacology) 2025: semaglutide and bone (OVX rats; BMSCs/Wnt). Sun 2025: semaglutide, metabolism, and gut microbiota in HFD mice. Qi 2025: GABA_B agonism vs GLP-1 RA chronotropy/arrhythmia post-MI. Yang 2026: electroacupuncture + semaglutide in T2D models. Khater 2026: *P. niruri* niosomes vs semaglutide in HFD rats (n=8/group); GLP-1R antagonist reversal.

**Vesicles.** Chen 2026: AD-MSC exosome / *Spirulina* hydrogel ± laser in diabetic rat wounds (animal n **not stated** in extract).

**Gap-fill.** Pechenov 2021: MEDI7219 oral GLP-1 RA tablets; ~6% bioavailability in dogs.

Each paper does **not** measure the others’ primary endpoints.

## (b) Themes the data support vs themes that would be forced

**Supported.**

1. **Human glycaemic evidence in the retrieved set is sparse and mixed:** one T1D AID crossover (Pasqua), one T2D real-world TIR study (Chen 2024), oral-product PK/BE (Ma, Nielsen), plus utilisation/FAERS/simulation papers. They cannot be pooled.
2. **Semaglutide safety at CV risk is represented by one large MA** (Sillassen), not by the delivery papers.
3. **Non-injectable GLP-1 exposure is an engineering scatter:** lung, skin, oral LNC/SEDDS, botanical niosomes, veterinary implant—different payloads, species, and endpoints.
4. **Extra-glycaemic biology is mechanistic** (bone, muscle, heart, brain review, hepatocytes).
5. **MSC-exosome therapy appears once** (Chen 2026 diabetic wounds), without GLP-1.

**Would be forced — do not write.**

- Pooling Jeong, Woo, Yao, Zhang, Domingues, Sandmeier, and Khater as one “delivery effect.”
- Treating Sillassen mortality RRs as safety of ZnO inhalation or exosome hydrogels.
- Using Pasqua T1D TIR as proof of oral or pulmonary GLP-1 RA efficacy.
- Citing the 139 unread OA titles as findings.
- Equating Pechenov’s ~6% dog BA for MEDI7219 with marketed oral semaglutide in humans.

## (c) Real disagreements and why

Almost entirely **incommensurable designs**, not conflicting point estimates. Pasqua (T1D AID TIR) vs Chen 2024 (T2D CGM TIR) differ in population and design. da Silva Lima (no direct hepatic GLP-1/GIP action) vs Domingues (semaglutide LNC in MASLD) are not the same experiment: in vitro human cells vs in vivo delivery aiming at native GLP-1 and absorbed analogue. Jeong’s <2% oral semaglutide remark is product literature; Pechenov measures a different molecule in dogs. Khater is GLP-1R-dependent botanical niosomes, not a licensed GLP-1 RA. Zhang is RA joints, not diabetes. Insulin-delivery papers (Fredholt, Yu, Singh) sit beside GLP-1 papers without sharing a payload.

## (d) What this sample cannot answer

- Human bioavailability of pulmonary liraglutide, microneedle semaglutide, or niosomal GLP-1R agonists.
- Field-wide CVOT evidence (SELECT/SUSTAIN-6 titles appeared on OpenAlex lists; PDFs were **not** fetched as a new systematic review).
- Whether MSC-exosome hydrogels heal diabetic wounds in patients; Chen’s animal n missing from extract.
- The content of 139 unretrieved OA records (including Huang 2026 IJPX, retried, still no PDF).
- Qiu 2026 BAF60a/GLP-1 (wrong file downloaded).
- Head-to-head clinical non-injectable vs SC GLP-1 RA PK.

## (e) Outline of the review

Argument: this OA export, after public-PDF retrieval, is a **translational scatter**. Human comparative evidence is thin (Pasqua; Sillassen; mixed real-world/PK papers). Delivery and vesicle work is mostly animal. Do not pool.

1. Abstract / keywords — 44 export papers + 1 gap-fill; 139 not retrieved; do not pool.
2. Introduction — question is what this export contains after OA fetch.
3. Methods — identification, eligibility, selection counts; no token meters.
4. Clinical incretin use (Pasqua, Chen 2024, Nielsen, Ma, Hirotsu, Swift, Xiong, Wang, Abdallah, Ali, Lunati, Fragozo-Ramos, Tian protocol).
5. Safety MA and narrative clinical reviews (Sillassen, Yue, Shen, Bonga, Son, Wallentowitz, Zachou, Hölscher).
6. Non-injectable and local delivery (Jeong, Woo, Yao, Zhang, Domingues, Sandmeier, Kweon, Dangi, Klotsman, Fredholt, Yu, Xu, Singh, Pechenov gap-fill, Khater).
7. Extra-glycaemic biology (da Silva Lima, Mastaitis, Abo-Elenin, Tian bone, Sun, Qi, Yang).
8. MSC-exosome diabetic wounds (Chen 2026); Raubenheimer as public-interest not efficacy.
9. Discussion — supported vs forced; 139-file hole; remaining gaps.
10. Conclusions / references — only extracted full texts.

Every included export paper appears in §§4–8. Pechenov appears in §6 only as gap-fill.
