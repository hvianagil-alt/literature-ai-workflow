## Research question
What would introducing SGLT2is and GLP-1 RAs do to insulin use and complications in LMICs?

## Methods
Microsimulation of introducing SGLT2 inhibitors and GLP-1 RAs in LMIC health systems.

## Sample / data
Simulated LMIC populations with diabetes; not a trial n.

## Claim-ready facts
- **Design:** microsimulation
- **Population / model:** low- and middle-income country diabetes care (modelled)
- **n:** simulation, not a trial n
- **Intervention / comparator:** introduction of SGLT2i + GLP-1 RA
- **Primary endpoint:** insulin dose and complications (modelled)
- **Primary result:** modelled effects on insulin use and complications
- **Cannot show:** a measured RCT effect in those countries

## Key findings
- Microsimulation of access/substitution, not a TIR trial.

## Limitations (as stated by the authors, or evident from the methods)
- Model assumptions; not empirical outcomes.

## Relevance to our research question
Access in LMICs.
