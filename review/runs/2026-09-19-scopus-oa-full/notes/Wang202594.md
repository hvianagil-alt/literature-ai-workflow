## Research question
Did SGLT2i and GLP-1 RA uptake among US Medicare beneficiaries with T2D plus cardiorenal disease differ by race/ethnicity in 2013–2019?

## Methods
Observational analysis of Medicare claims 2013–2019.

## Sample / data
Older adults with T2D plus HF, ASCVD, or CKD.

- **Population / model:** US Medicare beneficiaries with T2D and cardiorenal conditions
- **n:** not restated as a single n in the article extract
