# Fabrication and Preclinical Evaluation of Hyaluronic Acid/Aminoclay Nanocomposite Microneedles for Noninvasive Delivery of Semaglutide in Anti-Obesity Therapy

- **Citation (as given in the paper / filename):** Woo, Chang Rim and Kim, Gyu Lin and Han, Hyo-Kyung, 2025, International Journal of Nanomedicine. doi:10.2147/IJN.S544952
- **Source file:** papers/scopus-oa-full/Woo202511843.pdf
- **Extracted:** 2026-09-19
- **Screening:** included after full text.

## Research question
Do HA/aminoclay microneedles deliver semaglutide better than free-drug needles in rats?

## Methods
Sema–aminoclay nanocomplex in HA dissolving needles (Sema-AC/HA10); reverse PDMS moulds.

## Sample / data
Rats (obesity formulation); group sizes not reported.

## Claim-ready facts
- **Design:** preclinical microneedle PK/PD
- **Population / model:** rats
- **n:** group sizes not reported
- **Intervention / comparator:** Sema-AC/HA10 vs free-drug needles
- **Primary endpoint:** mechanical strength, stability, systemic exposure, glucose/weight
- **Primary result:** 0.37 ± 0.020 N/needle; stable at 25 °C; greater exposure than free drug; metabolic reductions in rats
- **Cannot show:** human bioequivalence

## Key findings
- Needle force 0.37 N; greater exposure than free-drug needles.

## Limitations (as stated by the authors, or evident from the methods)
- n not reported; preclinical.
