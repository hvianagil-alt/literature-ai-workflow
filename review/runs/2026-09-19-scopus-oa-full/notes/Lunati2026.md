# Case Report: Latent autoimmune diabetes in two young female patients successfully treated with oral semaglutide and basal insulin

- **Extracted:** 2026-09-19
- **Screening:** included after full text.

## Research question
Can oral semaglutide plus basal insulin be used in latent autoimmune diabetes of youth?

## Methods
Case report of two young women.

## Sample / data
Two patients (onset 8–29 years); one described as age 20, BMI 31.8 kg·m⁻², residual C-peptide, no ketoacidosis at presentation.

- **Design:** case report
- **Population / model:** latent autoimmune diabetes of youth (LADY)
- **n:** 2
- **Intervention / comparator:** oral semaglutide + basal insulin
- **Primary endpoint:** clinical course (not a primary trial endpoint)
- **Primary result:** both treated; residual C-peptide present in the detailed case
