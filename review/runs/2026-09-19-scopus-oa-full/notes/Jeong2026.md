# Morphology-driven zinc oxide biointeractive carriers with biological barrier modulating effects for pulmonary delivery of liraglutide

- **Citation (as given in the paper / filename):** Jeong, Jin-Hyuk and Han, Chang-Soo and Kang, Ji-Hyun and Kim, Dong-Wook and Park, Chun-Woong, 2026, Journal of Nanobiotechnology. doi:10.1186/s12951-026-04085-y
- **Source file:** papers/scopus-oa-full/Jeong2026.pdf
- **Extracted:** 2026-09-19
- **Screening:** included after full text.

## Research question
Does zinc-oxide particle morphology change pulmonary liraglutide bioavailability vs SC injection?

## Methods
Three ZO morphologies; PIV aerodynamics; intratracheal liraglutide vs SC in rats.

## Sample / data
Healthy and alloxan-diabetic rats; animal n not reported in the article extract.

## Claim-ready facts
- **Design:** preclinical pulmonary PK/formulation
- **Population / model:** healthy and alloxan-diabetic rats
- **n:** not reported in the extract used for the article
- **Intervention / comparator:** intratracheal liraglutide on ZO-3 vs SC; ZO-1/2/3 morphologies
- **Primary endpoint:** relative bioavailability vs SC
- **Primary result:** ~60% healthy rats; ~51% diabetic rats for ZO-3; <2% oral semaglutide contrast is product literature, not same-animal oral arm
- **Cannot show:** human inhaled liraglutide PK

## Key findings
- ZO-3 favoured distal deposition; relative BA ~60%/51% vs SC.

## Limitations (as stated by the authors, or evident from the methods)
- Rats; n not reported; oral contrast not in-study.

## Relevance to our research question
Strongest pulmonary PK number in the sample.
