# Novel approach for diabetic wound healing: adipose-derived mesenchymal stromal cells Exo@SPHydrogel combined with laser therapy

- **Citation (as given in the paper / filename):** Chen, Weizhi and Hong, Jie and Wei, Yiliu and Ye, Junchen and Wu, Qiaoyi, 2026, npj Regenerative Medicine. doi:10.1038/s41536-026-00459-w
- **Source file:** papers/scopus-oa-full/Chen2026.pdf
- **Extracted:** 2026-09-19
- **Screening:** included after full text.

## Research question
Do AD-MSC exosomes in a Spirulina hydrogel plus laser heal diabetic wounds?

## Methods
AD-MSC-exo@SP hydrogel ± laser; transcriptome of diabetic rat wound tissue; biofilm, M2, angiogenesis.

## Sample / data
Diabetic rat wounds; group sizes not reported.

## Claim-ready facts
- **Design:** preclinical EV + device
- **Population / model:** diabetic rat wounds
- **n:** group sizes not reported
- **Intervention / comparator:** AD-MSC-exo@SP ± laser
- **Primary endpoint:** wound closure; endothelial/M2 programmes
- **Primary result:** accelerated closure; laser combination strongest; fewer endothelial cells and M2 on transcriptome of untreated diabetic wounds
- **Cannot show:** incretin class effect; human wounds

## Key findings
- No GLP-1 payload; vesicle-plus-device for a diabetic complication.

## Limitations (as stated by the authors, or evident from the methods)
- n not reported; not an incretin formulation.

## Relevance to our research question
Do not pool with AID or pulmonary PK.
