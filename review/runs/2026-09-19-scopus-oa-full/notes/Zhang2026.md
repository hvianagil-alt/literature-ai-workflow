- **Citation (as given in the paper / filename):** Zhang, Hongyu and liu, Yao and Zhang, Shiyu and Wang, Zuli and Zhang, Xia and Zhang, Yang and Zhang, Ji and Liang, Shenju and Li, Changqing and Su, Min and Tian, Zhiqiang and Luo, Liwen, 2026, Journal of Nanobiotechnology. doi:10.1186/s12951-026-04261-0

## Research question
Can MN-delivered silk-fibroin nanoparticles of GLP-1 RAs treat collagen-induced arthritis?

## Methods
Silk-fibroin NPs (EE ~28–33%; ~60% released by day 14), WYRGRL decoration, dissolving MNs, CIA rats.

## Sample / data
CIA rats; sample size not reported in the article extract.

## Claim-ready facts
- **Design:** preclinical articular MN
- **Population / model:** collagen-induced arthritis rats
- **n:** not reported
- **Intervention / comparator:** GLP-1 RA NPs in MNs vs articular exposure problem
- **Primary endpoint:** arthritis / ferroptosis / M2 polarisation (proposed)
- **Primary result:** formulation numbers EE 28–33% and ~60% release by day 14; not a diabetes TIR
- **Cannot show:** diabetes delivery effect

## Key findings
- RA joints, not diabetes; EE ~28–33%.

## Limitations (as stated by the authors, or evident from the methods)
- n not reported; different disease.
