# Drug release profile of a novel exenatide long-term drug delivery system (OKV-119) administered to cats

- **Citation (as given in the paper / filename):** Klotsman, Michael and Anderson, Wayne H. and Gilor, Chen, 2024, BMC Veterinary Research. doi:10.1186/s12917-024-04051-6
- **Source file:** papers/scopus-oa-full/Klotsman2024.pdf
- **Extracted:** 2026-09-19
- **Screening:** included after full text.

## Research question
What is the 84-day release profile of an exenatide implant (OKV-119) in cats?

## Methods
Long-term SC implant OKV-119; plasma exenatide over 112 days.

## Sample / data
Five purpose-bred cats; four of five showed reduced intake/weight over first 28 days.

## Claim-ready facts
- **Design:** veterinary implant PK/PD
- **Population / model:** purpose-bred cats
- **n:** 5 (112-day follow-up)
- **Intervention / comparator:** OKV-119 exenatide implant
- **Primary endpoint:** plasma exenatide; intake/weight
- **Primary result:** detectable from day 7; above baseline beyond day 84; intake/weight down in 4/5 over 28 days
- **Cannot show:** human GLP-1 RA TIR

## Key findings
- n=5 cats; plasma drug beyond day 84.

## Limitations (as stated by the authors, or evident from the methods)
- Veterinary; n=5.

## Relevance to our research question
