# Effects of semaglutide-loaded lipid nanocapsules on metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease

- **Citation (as given in the paper / filename):** Domingues, Inês and Yagoubi, Hafsa and Zhang, Wunan and Marotti, Valentina and Kambale, Espoir K. and Vints, Katlijn and Sliwinska, Malgorzata Alicja and Leclercq, Isabelle A. and Beloqui, Ana, 2024, Drug Delivery and Translational Research. doi:10.1007/s13346-024-01576-z

- **Extracted:** 2026-09-19
- **Screening:** included after full text.

## Research question
Do oral semaglutide lipid nanocapsules improve MASLD more than marketed oral semaglutide by raising native GLP-1 and absorbed analogue?

## Methods
Oral semaglutide-loaded LNCs in MASLD models.

## Sample / data
MASLD models (preclinical); human therapeutic BA not reported.

## Claim-ready facts
- **Design:** preclinical oral nanocarrier
- **Population / model:** MASLD animal/models
- **n:** not reported as a single n in the article
- **Intervention / comparator:** semaglutide LNCs vs marketed oral semaglutide (design aim)
- **Primary endpoint:** metabolic and histologic MASLD effect
- **Primary result:** strategy tests local gut + absorbed analogue; complementary to no direct hepatocyte action (da Silva Lima)
- **Cannot show:** human therapeutic bioavailability

## Key findings
- Delivery aimed at native GLP-1 and analogue; not a hepatocyte-dish replica.

## Limitations (as stated by the authors, or evident from the methods)
- Preclinical; human BA not reported.

## Relevance to our research question
MASLD delivery vs direct hepatic agonism.
