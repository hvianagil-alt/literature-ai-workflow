## Key findings
- Lead/abstract as extracted: Obesity is a complex, multifactorial disease that contributes to a broad range of cardiometabolic, reproductive, and psychological disorders. Representing a major global health challenge, obesity can be addressed by lifestyle modifications such as reduced calorie intake, physical activity, adequate sleep, and stress manage- ment to help achieve sustainable weight loss and improve metabolic health in the long term. Glucose - dependent insulinotropic polypeptide (GIP) and glucagon -like peptide-1 (GLP-1) are the two naturally produced incretin hormones in the gastrointestinal tract. Incretin analogues were initially approved for type 2 diabetes mellitus but were later found to exhibit weight-reducing properties. Liraglutide, semaglutide, and tirzepatide are the three incretin analogues approved for obesity in non -diabetic patients. This narrative review presents detailed comparisons of the three approved incretin analogues for obesity, their cost - effectiveness, and trends in the clinical setting.
