# The adverse effects associated with semaglutide use in patients at increased risk of cardiovascular events: a systematic review with meta-analysis and Trial Sequential Analysis

- **Citation (as given in the paper / filename):** Sillassen, Christina Dam Bjerregaard and Petersen, Johanne Juul and Faltermeier, Pascal and Yucel, Delal and Siddiqui, Faiza and Andersen, Rebecca Kjær and Graever, Leonardo and Bjerg, Jonas Leth and Kamp, Caroline Barkholt and Grand, Johannes and Dominguez, Helena and Frølich, Anne and Gæde, Peter and Gluud, Christian and Mathiesen, Ole and Jakobsen, Janus Christian, 2025, BMC Medicine. doi:10.1186/s12916-025-04486-0
- **Source file:** papers/scopus-oa-full/Sillassen2025.pdf
- **Extracted:** 2026-09-19
- **Screening:** included after full text.

## Research question
What adverse effects (and mortality/MI) are associated with semaglutide vs placebo in people at increased CV risk?

## Methods
Systematic review, meta-analysis, Trial Sequential Analysis; Cochrane RoB 2 and GRADE; search to 31 March 2025, six databases.

## Sample / data
50 randomised placebo-controlled trials; 54,972 randomised (30,281 semaglutide, 24,691 placebo).

## Claim-ready facts
- **Design:** systematic review and meta-analysis
- **Population / model:** people at increased cardiovascular risk (mixed T2D/overweight/CKD)
- **n:** 50 trials; 54,972 randomised
- **Intervention / comparator:** oral or SC semaglutide vs placebo
- **Primary endpoint:** all-cause mortality, MI, SAEs, GI AEs
- **Primary result:** mortality RR 0.85 (0.79–0.91; I²=0%); MI RR 0.77 (0.69–0.85); SAE RR 0.93 (0.88–0.98); nausea RR 3.00; vomiting RR 4.12; diarrhoea RR 1.88
- **Cannot show:** safety of inhaled ZnO liraglutide, joint microneedles, or EV hydrogels

## Key findings
- Largest pooled human safety estimates in this sample; apply to CV-risk populations vs placebo.

## Limitations (as stated by the authors, or evident from the methods)
- Mixed routes and diagnoses; not a delivery-system safety envelope.

## Relevance to our research question
Human CV-risk safety anchor.
