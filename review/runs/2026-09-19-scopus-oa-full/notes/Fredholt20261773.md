# Impact of sodium caprate dosed as a mini-tablet or suspension on insulin delivery and mucosa histomorphology

- **Citation (as given in the paper / filename):** Fredholt, Freja and Heade, Joanne and Rantanen, Jukka and Rønholt, Stine and Nielsen, Hanne Mørck, 2026, Drug Delivery and Translational Research. doi:10.1007/s13346-025-01977-8
- **Source file:** papers/scopus-oa-full/Fredholt20261773.pdf
- **Extracted:** 2026-09-19
- **Screening:** included after full text.

## Research question
Does sodium caprate as a mini-tablet vs suspension change insulin delivery and mucosa?

## Methods
Intestinal insulin + fixed 26 mg·kg⁻¹ C10 as mini-tablet vs liquid in rats.

## Sample / data
Rats; 75 IU·kg⁻¹ mini-tablet condition highlighted.

## Claim-ready facts
- **Design:** preclinical oral insulin + PE
- **Population / model:** rats
- **n:** not restated as a single n in the article
- **Intervention / comparator:** C10 mini-tablet vs liquid C10 with insulin
- **Primary endpoint:** insulin bioavailability; mucosa histomorphology
- **Primary result:** 75 IU·kg⁻¹ mini-tablet increased BA 26-fold; transient mucosal erosion similar to liquid C10
- **Cannot show:** GLP-1 RA TIR

## Key findings
- Solid vs liquid PE comparable erosion; BA ↑26-fold at 75 IU·kg⁻¹.

## Limitations (as stated by the authors, or evident from the methods)
- Insulin payload, not GLP-1 RA; rats.

## Relevance to our research question
Oral peptide tablet PE, different payload.
