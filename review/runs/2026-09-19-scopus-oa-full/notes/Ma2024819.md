# Effect of Food Consumption on the Pharmacokinetics, Safety, and Tolerability of Once-Daily Orally Administered Orforglipron (LY3502970), a Non-peptide GLP-1 Receptor Agonist

- **Citation (as given in the paper / filename):** Ma, Xiaosu and Liu, Rong and Pratt, Edward J. and Benson, Charles T. and Bhattachar, Shobha N. and Sloop, Kyle W., 2024, Diabetes Therapy. doi:10.1007/s13300-024-01554-1
- **Source file:** papers/scopus-oa-full/Ma2024819.pdf
- **Extracted:** 2026-09-19
- **Screening:** included after full text.

## Research question
Does food change orforglipron AUC and Cmax?

## Methods
Two phase 1 food-effect crossovers (NCT03929744, NCT05110794).

## Sample / data
Healthy adults; n=12 after 3 mg; n=34 after multiple 16 mg.

## Claim-ready facts
- **Design:** phase 1 food-effect crossover
- **Population / model:** healthy adults
- **n:** 12 (single 3 mg); 34 (multiple 16 mg)
- **Intervention / comparator:** fed vs fasted orforglipron (non-peptide GLP-1 RA)
- **Primary endpoint:** AUC and Cmax
- **Primary result:** fed AUC/Cmax 23.7%/23.2% lower (3 mg) and 17.6%/20.9% lower (16 mg); t½ and tmax comparable
- **Cannot show:** HbA1c vs oral semaglutide

## Key findings
- Food lowered exposure by about one-fifth; GI events predominated; no deaths reported.

## Limitations (as stated by the authors, or evident from the methods)
- Phase 1; not an efficacy trial vs SNAC tablets.

## Relevance to our research question
Small-molecule oral GLP-1 RA PK.
