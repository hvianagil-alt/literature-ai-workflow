# GDF8 and activin A blockade protects against GLP-1–induced muscle loss while enhancing fat loss in obese male mice and non-human primates

- **Citation (as given in the paper / filename):** Mastaitis, Jason W. and Gomez, Daniel and Raya, José G. and Li, Diana and Min, Soo and Stec, Michael and Kleiner, Sandra and McWilliams, Toya and Altarejos, Judith Y. and Murphy, Andrew J. and Yancopoulos, George D. and Sleeman, Mark W., 2025, Nature Communications. doi:10.1038/s41467-025-59485-9
- **Source file:** papers/scopus-oa-full/Mastaitis2025.pdf
- **Extracted:** 2026-09-19
- **Screening:** included after full text.

## Research question
Does GDF8 and activin A blockade spare muscle during GLP-1 RA treatment while enhancing fat loss?

## Methods
Dual blockade of GDF8 and activin A with GLP-1 RA in obese male mice and non-human primates.

## Sample / data
Obese male mice and NHPs; human body-composition RCT not reported.

## Claim-ready facts
- **Design:** preclinical combination PD
- **Population / model:** obese male mice and non-human primates
- **n:** not restated as a single n in the article
- **Intervention / comparator:** GDF8/activin A blockade + GLP-1 RA vs GLP-1 RA
- **Primary endpoint:** muscle mass; fat loss
- **Primary result:** prevented GLP-1-associated muscle loss; increased muscle; increased fat loss
- **Cannot show:** human lean mass/function RCT

## Key findings
- Muscle spared in mouse and NHP; human RCT not reported.

## Limitations (as stated by the authors, or evident from the methods)
- Not human.
