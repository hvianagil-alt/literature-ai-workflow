# Effect of High-Pressure Processing on Physico-Chemical, Microbiological and Sensory Traits in Fresh Fish Fillets (*Salmo salar* and *Pleuronectes platessa*)

- **Citation (as given in the paper / filename):** Castrica M, Pavlovic R, Balzaretti CM, Curone G, Brecchia G, Copelotti E, Panseri S, Pessina D, Arnoldi C, Chiesa LM. Foods. 2021;10(8):1775. doi:10.3390/foods10081775. PMC8392521.
- **Source file:** papers/2026-09-20-hpp-rerun/Castrica2021.txt
- **Extracted:** 2026-09-20

## Research question
What does a single HPP cycle of 500 MPa for 2 min at 4 °C do to microbiology, volatiles, histamine, colour, pH, metabolome, and sensory traits of vacuum-skin-packed salmon and plaice fillets during refrigerated storage?

## Methods
Split-plot storage study. Fresh *Salmo salar* (~250 g) and *Pleuronectes platessa* (~100 g) fillets in vacuum skin packaging: untreated controls vs HPP 500 MPa/2 min/4 °C. Storage at 4 °C; sampling on D0, D3, D6, D8, and D12. Untreated fillets analysed only through D8 (hygiene cut-off); HPP fillets through D12. Endpoints: HS-SPME-GC-MS volatiles; HPLC-Q-Exactive-Orbitrap metabolomics and histamine (D0 metabolomics only); Enterobacteriaceae, *Pseudomonas* spp., mesophilic and psychrotrophic counts (Log CFU/g); CIE L*a*b*; pH; 9-point sensory scores (appearance, odour, texture) by five laboratory judges. Nonparametric Mann–Whitney / Wilcoxon tests; p ≤ 0.05.

## Sample / data
n = 3 fillets per group per time point for physico-chemical, microbiological, and sensory analyses (triplicate biological replicates for metabolomics and VOCs). Analyses in duplicate for microbiology and colour (5 colour readings averaged).

## Claim-ready facts
- **Design:** controlled storage experiment (HPP vs untreated vacuum-packed fillets)
- **Population / model:** fresh Atlantic salmon and plaice fillets
- **n:** 3 fillets per treatment × species × time point
- **Intervention / comparator:** 500 MPa for 2 min at 4 °C vs untreated vacuum skin packaging; 4 °C storage
- **Primary endpoint:** microbial counts (Log CFU/g of Enterobacteriaceae, *Pseudomonas* spp., mesophiles, psychrotrophs) plus colour (L*), sensory appearance/texture/odour, histamine, sulfur VOCs
- **Primary result:** HPP reduced growth of every counted group at all times (p ≤ 0.05) and kept treated fillets microbiologically acceptable to D12 versus D8 for controls. Histamine in HPP fish did not rise through storage except low concentrations at D12, still far below the 100 µg g^−1 legal limit (Regulation EC 1441/2007); control histamine rose at D6 and D8 (p < 0.01) without matching microbial change. L* increased immediately: salmon D0 70.25 ± 5.06 (HPP) vs 44.91 ± 2.57 (control); plaice D0 79.50 ± 3.06 vs 62.14 ± 3.73 (p = 0.01 at each time). Appearance scores collapsed after HPP (salmon D0 3.18 ± 0.06 vs 8.31 ± 0.08; plaice D0 2.77 ± 0.14 vs 5.40 ± 0.25). Texture scores likewise (salmon D0 2.49 ± 0.04 vs 8.11 ± 0.11; plaice D0 3.59 ± 0.03 vs 8.31 ± 0.43). Odour was stabilised: salmon D6 8.38 ± 0.06 (HPP) vs 5.64 ± 0.74; plaice D6 6.66 ± 0.37 vs 3.50 ± 0.30 (p ≤ 0.05). Sulfur VOCs and ketones (2,3-butanedione, 3-hydroxy-2-butanone) were prevented (p ≤ 0.05 or p < 0.01). Alanyl-tyrosine was upregulated in both species after 500 MPa.
- **Cannot show:** pathogen challenge or spore kill; a 5-log pasteurization claim; consumer purchase tests; that 500 MPa/2 min is interchangeable with milder cycles for raw-fish appearance.

## Key findings
- Measurement (Table 2): no consistent HPP effect on pH (salmon D0 6.51 ± 0.20 HPP vs 5.89 ± 0.18 control; plaice D0 6.62 ± 0.34 vs 6.70 ± 0.05).
- Measurement/interpretation: plaice HPP upregulated AMP and downregulated IMP, which the authors interpret as AMP deaminase inhibition with alkaline/acid phosphatase still active (untargeted metabolomics; proposed, not a direct enzyme assay).
- Author interpretation: 500 MPa/2 min/4 °C behaves like a bacteriostat and extends commercial storage, but fillets look cooked (opaque, compact), which may hurt retail of raw fish (Conclusions).

## Limitations (as stated by the authors, or evident from the methods)
- Sensory panel n = 5 laboratory staff (COVID-19 restriction); 9-point scale, not a consumer test.
- Exact Log CFU/g values are in figures, not tabulated in the text.
- Exact histamine µg g^−1 values are in Figure 5, not tabulated.
- One pressure–time–temperature combination; authors note 100–300 MPa cycles in other studies may spare appearance.

## Relevance to our research question
A named 500 MPa/2 min/4 °C cycle in fish is **not** the same quality outcome as untreated raw fillets: microbial shelf life extends to 12 days and sulfur off-odours/histamine are delayed, yet L* jumps and appearance/texture scores fall into the cooked/opaque range. Safety gain and raw-fish quality are therefore uncoupled at this cycle.
