# Quality of Refrigerated Squid Mantle Cut Treated with Mint Extract Subjected to High-Pressure Processing

- **Citation (as given in the paper / filename):** Nilsuwan K, Palamae S, Naher J, Buamard N, Zhang B, Benjakul S (2024). Foods 13:1264. doi:10.3390/foods13081264
- **Source file:** papers/2026-09-20-hpp-rerun/Nilsuwan2024.txt
- **Extracted:** 2026-09-20

## Research question
Do mint extract (ME) and HPP, alone or combined, extend refrigerated shelf life of squid mantle cut (SMC) by lowering microbial counts, volatile bases, lipid oxidation, drip/cooking loss, and texture loss, and which spoilage taxa remain after the strongest hurdle?

## Methods
Completely randomized design. Planktonic *Pseudomonas aeruginosa* ATCC 27834 and *Shewanella* spp. TBRC 5775 challenged with ME 400 mg/L (time-kill 0–24 h) or HPP 400 MPa for 0, 1, 3, 5, 7 min. SMC (*Loligo vulgaris*, 25.0 ± 0.5 g squid) treated with ME 0, 200, or 400 mg/L, vacuum packed, then HPP 200 or 400 MPa for 5 min (Jiujin 5 L, water medium), stored at 4 °C for 15 days. Codes: CON; M0/M2/M4 × P2 (200 MPa) or P4 (400 MPa). TVC, PBC, Pseudomonas, H2S-producers, Enterobacteriaceae; TVB/TMA; pH; PV/TBARS; weight and cooking loss; Warner-Bratzler firmness/toughness; 9-point hedonic sensory (n = 50) on day 0 (CON and M4P4) and day 12 (M4P4 only if TVC < 6 log CFU/g); 16S NGS on fresh, CON day 3, M4P4 day 12. Triplicate analyses; Tukey p < 0.05.

## Sample / data
Freshly caught squid, processed within ~13 h of harvest. Microbial assays n = 3. Sensory 50 untrained panelists. NGS: three designated samples (Fresh, CON day 3, M4P4 day 12).

## Claim-ready facts

- **Design:** Laboratory hurdle experiment (ME × HPP) with storage at 4 °C plus planktonic inactivation assays and 16S community profiling.
- **Population / model:** Squid mantle cut (*Loligo vulgaris*); planktonic *P. aeruginosa* and *Shewanella* spp. in broth.
- **n:** Quality/microbiology in triplicate; sensory n = 50; planktonic assays n = 3.
- **Intervention / comparator:** ME 0/200/400 mg/L then HPP 200 or 400 MPa, 5 min vs untreated CON. Planktonic HPP 400 MPa, 0–7 min.
- **Primary endpoint:** TVC (log CFU/g) vs 6 log CFU/g limit during 15 days at 4 °C; supporting TVB (mg N/100 g), texture, sensory, NGS relative abundance.
- **Primary result:** ME 400 mg/L reduced planktonic PA and *Shewanella* by 2.67 and 2.58 log CFU/mL over 24 h (end counts 2.10 and 2.34 log CFU/mL). HPP 400 MPa for 5 min reduced planktonic PA and *Shewanella* to 1.88 and 2.09 log CFU/mL (from ~7 log; 7 min 1.75 and 2.02, not different from 5 min). Day 0 TVC: CON 2.98 log CFU/g; all 400 MPa SMC samples ND. After day 3, HPP-treated TVC increased (reversible injury). Day 6: CON, M0P2, and M0P4 TVC exceeded the 6 log CFU/g limit. Day 12 M4P4 TVC 6.26 log CFU/g, the lowest among treatments (p < 0.05). Day 0 TVB 2.11–2.35 mg N/100 g all samples; day 6 CON 25.87 mg N/100 g; day 12 M4P4 TVB 15.21 vs M2P2 20.00, M4P2 21.23, M2P4 18.00. Day 15 M4P4 TMA 7.18 mg N/100 g; pH 7.45 (lowest); PV 8.01 vs CON 21.25 mg cumene hydroperoxide/kg; TBARS 0.42 mg MDA eq/kg (M2P4 0.61). Day 15 weight loss M4P4 7.18% vs CON 11.67%; cooking loss table 19.71 ± 0.73% (text states 19.17%). Day 0 firmness 7005.30–7658.84 g; day 15 CON 1768.10 g vs M4P4 2868.00 g. Day 0 overall likeness CON 8.61 ± 0.19 vs M4P4 8.67 ± 0.21 (ns); day 12 M4P4 overall 6.45. NGS: M4P4 day 12 dominated by *Brochothrix campestris* (96.84% of species; Listeriaceae 96.85%).
- **Cannot show:** Pathogen challenge (*Listeria*, *Salmonella*, *Vibrio*) in the squid matrix. HPP alone at 400 MPa/5 min does not keep TVC below 6 log through day 6. NGS is relative abundance, not counts. Enzyme inactivation is inferred, not assayed.

## Key findings
- Named 400 MPa/5 min is not the same as 200 MPa/5 min, and neither matches 400 MPa plus 400 mg/L ME: HPP alone failed the 6 log TVC limit by day 6; M4P4 delayed spoilage to about day 12. (Section 3.3.1)
- Measurement: Planktonic 400 MPa inactivation plateaued between 5 and 7 min. (Section 3.2)
- Author interpretation: ME phenolics plus HPP cell damage are combined hurdles; HPP-injured cells can recover during storage. (Section 3.3.1)
- Mechanism grade: protease/oxidase inactivation proposed from texture and TBARS; not directly measured. *B. campestris* survival after M4P4 is a measured community result. (Sections 3.3.6, 3.4)

## Limitations (as stated by the authors, or evident from the methods)
- CON at day 12 was not sensory-tested because TVC exceeded the limit, so storage sensory comparison is one-sided.
- Cooking-loss day-15 M4P4 differs between table (19.71%) and narrative (19.17%).
- Species spelling *Loligo vulguris* in methods; abstract uses *Loligo vulgaris*.
- No commercial HPP validation or frozen-chain comparison.

## Relevance to our research question
Strong matrix evidence that a named 400 MPa/5 min cycle is not a fixed safety outcome: without ME it does not hold squid TVC to 6 log past day 6, and survivors shift to *Brochothrix campestris*. Quality (TVB, drip, firmness, likeness) tracks the same hurdle interaction.
