# Strategies for effective high pressure germination or inactivation of Bacillus spores involving nisin

- **Citation (as given in the paper / filename):** Heydenreich R, Delbrück AI, Trunet C, Mathys A. Appl Environ Microbiol. 2024;90(10):e02299-23. doi:10.1128/aem.02299-23. PMC11505639. PMID:39311577. Received 19 Dec 2023; accepted 10 Aug 2024; published 23 Sep 2024.
- **Source file:** papers/2026-09-20-hpp-rerun-gapfill/Heydenreich2024_29923.txt
- **Extracted:** 2026-09-20

## Research question
Can moderate HP (mHP) and/or very high HP (vHP) at ≤80 °C, with nisin and nutrient germinants, germinate then inactivate *Bacillus* spores enough for a mild sterilization strategy—and is nisin a germination promoter or only an inactivator of already germinated spores?

## Methods
Isostatic HP in 50 mM ACES buffer pH 7.0 (not a food). mHP: 150 MPa, 37 °C, 5 min. vHP: 550 MPa, 60 °C, 2.5 min (also 9 min; 20 min for *B. amyloliquefaciens* in File S1). Combinations: 100 mM L-valine or L-alanine, AGFK, TSB (½ strength), nisin 250 or 2,500 IU/mL; mHP then vHP; optional 80 °C/10 min heat activation then 37 °C/6 h at atmospheric pressure then a second vHP. After treatment: 3× wash in ACES, plate count before and after 80 °C/20 min (selects ungerminated spores), FCM with SYTO16 + PI. NoHP controls on ice. Experimental replicates on ≥3 days (n ≥ 3); no inferential statistics (authors: small n). Nisin MIC in TSB: 2 technical wells × 3 days.

## Sample / data
*Bacillus subtilis* PS533 (strain 168 derivative, kanR plasmid); two spore batches; >97% dormant by FCM; N0 typically 10^9 CFU/mL (one batch 10^10). *Bacillus amyloliquefaciens* TMW 2.479 (Fad 82, ropy bread isolate); one batch; 89% dormant by FCM; N0 10^8 CFU/mL. Buffer pH 7.0, not a human food matrix.

## Claim-ready facts
- **Design:** laboratory spore germination–inactivation factorial study (plate count + flow cytometry)
- **Population / model:** dormant *B. subtilis* PS533 and HP-resistant *B. amyloliquefaciens* TMW 2.479 spores in ACES buffer pH 7.0
- **n:** independent experimental replicates n ≥ 3 (mostly n = 3); FCM three experimental replicates; MIC n = 2 × 3
- **Intervention / comparator:** NoHP vs mHP (150 MPa, 37 °C, 5 min) vs vHP (550 MPa, 60 °C, 2.5 or 9 min) ± nisin (250 or 2,500 IU/mL) ± L-valine/L-alanine (100 mM) or TSB; some arms two vHP cycles with 37 °C/6 h in between
- **Primary endpoint:** log10 (N/N0) of culturable spores before 80 °C/20 min (inactivation of germinated cells) and after heat (remaining culturable dormant/ungerminated spores); FCM % in ungerminated (R1), germinated intact (R2), PI-positive (R3)
- **Primary result:** NoHP reference: log10 reduction after heat ≈ 0. NoHP + 2,500 IU/mL nisin: −0.9 ± 0.3 before heat and −0.9 ± 0.1 after heat (Table 1; N0 10^9 CFU/mL). vHP 2.5 min alone (*B. subtilis*): −2.1 ± 0.3 before heat, −5.7 ± 0.2 after heat. vHP + 250 IU/mL nisin: −3.6 ± 0.3 / −5.8 ± 0.3. vHP + 2,500 IU/mL nisin: −5.3 ± 0.2 / −5.7 ± 0.1. After-heat reductions were similar with or without nisin (~5.7–5.8), so nisin increased inactivation of germinated spores, not the number that germinated. mHP alone: about −1 log culturable dormant spores; valine did not add kill; valine + nisin added ~1 further log on plates but FCM still showed ~6% ungerminated (nisin carry-over, not extra germination). Most effective *B. subtilis* combinations: nisin + L-valine or TSB + first vHP (550 MPa, 60 °C, 2.5 min) + 37 °C/6 h + second vHP — about 8 log10 reduction of culturable dormant spores, near the detection limit; still estimated <40 of 10^9 spores/mL remained ungerminated. Same 8-log-class result with or without 80 °C/10 min heat activation and with valine or TSB. “Val Nis mHP vHP” ≈ −7 log, similar to vHP alone for 9 min. *B. amyloliquefaciens*: vHP 2.5 min <1 log; vHP 20 min <2 log; the best *B. subtilis* combination (Val Nis vHP 37 °C vHP) only ~2 log. Growth of *B. subtilis* inhibited by ≥250 IU/mL nisin at 18 h or 2,500 IU/mL at 3 days; *B. amyloliquefaciens* inhibited by 2,500 IU/mL at 18 h and 3 days. Authors: no HP process has achieved food sterilization at ≤80 °C (Introduction).
- **Cannot show:** sterilization of a human food (buffer only); *C. botulinum* or other Clostridium spores; that 2,500 IU/mL nisin is a legal food dose (authors say approved levels are much lower); that nisin promotes HP germination under these conditions; complete germination of either species.

## Key findings
- Measurement: vHP 550 MPa/60 °C/2.5 min germinated a large *B. subtilis* fraction (~5.7 log after heat) but only partly inactivated them without nisin (−2.1 log before heat) (Table 1; Fig. 2).
- Measurement: adding 2,500 IU/mL nisin to vHP moved before-heat kill to −5.3 log while after-heat stay ~−5.7, i.e. nisin inactivated germinated spores rather than raising germination (Table 1; Abstract).
- Measurement: two vHP cycles + nutrient + nisin + 37 °C hold reached ~8 log reduction of culturable dormant *B. subtilis* spores, still not complete (Results).
- Measurement: the same named combination was not the same outcome in *B. amyloliquefaciens* (~2 log vs ~8 log) (Abstract; Fig. 3).
- Author interpretation: nisin did seemingly **not** promote HP germination initiation under the chosen HP conditions, contrary to some earlier literature (Abstract; Discussion vs Black et al.).
- Proposed mechanism (cited physiology + FCM): mHP acts via germinant receptors; vHP via DPA release independent of receptors; nisin pores in the inner membrane after germination (PI-positive R3 increases with nisin). *B. amyloliquefaciens* resistance possibly linked to *spoVA2mob*/2Duf (proposed, not measured here).
- Measurement caveat: nisin was not completely removed by washing (including acidic pH 2.5); plate counts can overstate germination if nisin remains.

## Limitations (as stated by the authors, or evident from the methods)
- Buffer pH 7.0, not a food; 2,500 IU/mL nisin is a mechanistic dose above regulatory food use.
- n mostly 3; no significance tests.
- Plate-count-with-heat is insufficient when nisin is present (Discussion).
- Complete germination not achieved; superdormant tail remains.
- One *B. amyloliquefaciens* spore batch.

## Relevance to our research question
**Gap-fill** paper retrieved after the literature table for the spore germination/inactivation under high pressure + nisin gap (Heydenreich2024_29923). Heat is not used as a classical 121 °C kill step (vHP at 60 °C; 80 °C used only to score ungerminated spores). A named vHP + nisin cycle is **not** one safety outcome: ~8 log culturable-dormant reduction in *B. subtilis* vs ~2 log in *B. amyloliquefaciens*, and nisin here is an inactivator of germinated spores, not a germination trigger. Not a human-food matrix.
