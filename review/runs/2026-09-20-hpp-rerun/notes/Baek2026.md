# Application of high hydrostatic pressure on the microbial and physicochemical characteristics of pork

- **Citation (as given in the paper / filename):** Baek UB, Kim HY. 2026. Food Science of Animal Resources 46:73. DOI:10.1007/s44463-026-00091-8
- **Source file:** papers/2026-09-20-hpp-rerun/Baek2026.txt (PDF also present)
- **Extracted:** 2026-09-20

## Research question

How do HHP pressure (0.1–500 MPa) and hold time (5 vs 15 min) change inoculated foodborne bacteria and physicochemical quality of raw pork loin during 2 weeks at refrigeration, and is 500 MPa/15 min a different safety and quality outcome from milder cycles?

## Methods

Pork loin (Daonchugsan, Yesan), 24 h post-slaughter at 4 °C; fat/connective tissue removed; vacuum packed. Physicochemical slices 1 cm; microbiology 25 g; composition 100 g; shear 600 g. Artificial contamination with multi-strain cocktails: E. coli (4 ATCC), B. cereus (2 ATCC), S. Typhimurium (3 ATCC), L. monocytogenes (3 ATCC), S. aureus (5 strains). Surface inoculum after attachment ~10^5–10^8 CFU/g. HHP: Uhde 055–60 (Korea Food Industry Cluster); external 25 °C, internal 21 °C; 100, 300, 500 MPa × 5 or 15 min vs 0.1 MPa control. Storage 0, 1, 2 weeks (physicochemical samples stored 0, 1, or 2 weeks before use is also stated). Counts on commercial films (Microfast / MC-Media Pads / 3M Petrifilm); 37 °C 24 h except B. cereus 30 °C 24 h; ND < 1 log CFU/g. Proximate AOAC; WHC planimeter; shear TA1 V-blade (kgf); pH; CIE colour after 30 min bloom; TBARS as mg MDA/kg; Conway VBN (mg%). ≥ triplicate; two-way ANOVA pressure × time; Tukey p < 0.05; storage analysed separately.

## Sample / data

Raw pork loin. Intended application stated as raw pet-food protein, but the matrix is pork and the inocula are human foodborne pathogens. Tables 2–3 captions in the extracted text say “beef” while the study is pork (likely a copy-edit error).

## Claim-ready facts

- **Design:** Factorial laboratory challenge plus quality assays on vacuum-packed raw pork (pressure × time × storage).
- **Population / model:** Pork loin inoculated with E. coli, B. cereus, S. Typhimurium, L. monocytogenes, and S. aureus cocktails; physicochemical assays on uninoculated or parallel loin (not stated as the same pieces).
- **n:** Experiments “at least in triplicate”; inoculum prepared to 10^7–10^9 CFU/ml, ~10^5–10^8 CFU/g on meat after attachment. Exact biological replicate count of loins not reported.
- **Intervention / comparator:** 100, 300, 500 MPa for 5 or 15 min versus 0.1 MPa control; 4 °C storage 0–2 weeks.
- **Primary endpoint:** Pathogen counts (log CFU/g); supporting moisture/protein/fat/ash (%), WHC (%), shear (kgf), pH, CIE L*a*b*, TBARS (mg MDA/kg), VBN (mg%).
- **Primary result:** Inactivation increased with pressure and time; 500 MPa/15 min strongest. S. Typhimurium and L. monocytogenes fell to ND (<1 log CFU/g) during storage after 500 MPa/15 min. B. cereus, E. coli, and S. aureus declined but remained detectable; S. aureus showed post-treatment increase during storage in the 500 MPa/15 min group (text). Exact log CFU/g values live in Figure 1 heatmap, not printed as a numeric table. Proximate (Table 1): moisture 70.71 ± 0.64% (control) to 74.25 ± 0.90% (500 MPa/15 min); protein 22.37 ± 0.10% to 24.13 ± 0.16%; fat 5.98 ± 0.14% to 0.22 ± 0.14%; ash 1.44 ± 0.02% to 1.04 ± 0.02%; WHC 30.73 ± 0.27% to 53.71 ± 0.33%; shear 2.54 ± 0.41 to 4.50 ± 0.85 kgf. pH day 0: control 6.796 ± 0.25 vs 500 MPa/15 min 6.946 ± 0.01. L* day 0: 53.78 ± 0.91 vs 78.88 ± 0.94. a* peaked at 300 MPa (day 0 3.98–4.02) vs control 0.82 ± 0.52; 500 MPa/15 min 3.58 ± 0.36. TBARS (Table 3): control week 0 0.04 ± 0.01 vs 500/15 min 0.06 ± 0.01; week 2 0.05 ± 0.01 vs 0.21 ± 0.02 mg MDA/kg. VBN week 2: control 4.68 ± 0.32 vs 500/15 min 3.18 ± 0.26 mg%. VBN increase rate 0–2 weeks: highest 100 MPa/5 min (111.72%), lowest 500 MPa/5 min (13.24%).
- **Cannot show:** Validated commercial pet-food or human-meat process; native (non-inoculated) microbiota only; spore inactivation of B. cereus (incomplete kill attributed to spores, not assayed as spores); sensory or feeding trials; whether “beef” table captions match pork data.

## Key findings

- Measurement: Pressure × time increased kill for all five taxa; 500 MPa/15 min put S. Typhimurium and L. monocytogenes below 1 log CFU/g during refrigerated storage (Microbial analysis; Figure 1).
- Measurement: L. monocytogenes (Gram-positive) reached ND while S. aureus did not; authors state inactivation is species-dependent, not strictly Gram type (same section).
- Measurement: Moisture, protein, WHC, shear, pH, L*, b*, and TBARS rose with intensity; fat, ash, and later VBN were lower at high pressure (Tables 1–3).
- Author interpretation: Proximate shifts are relative (water/protein network vs purge of fat/minerals), not covalent-bond changes (Campus 2010 cited) (Proximate composition).
- Proposed mechanisms (not directly measured here): membrane compression, phospholipid disruption, myofibrillar denaturation/aggregation, heme-iron release promoting oxidation, enzyme inactivation reducing VBN (Discussion/Conclusion).
- Author conclusion: 500 MPa/15 min is not a stand-alone complete kill; paler colour and higher TBARS; hurdle strategies needed (Conclusion).

## Limitations (as stated by the authors, or evident from the methods)

- Authors: incomplete elimination; paler appearance and increased lipid oxidation; recommend salt, antimicrobials, packaging, cold chain (Conclusion).
- Framed for raw pet food (FDA/AVMA zoonosis context) even though pork loin is a human-food matrix.
- Protocol of this review excludes pet-food papers; screening still listed this citekey as included — flag for synthesizer.
- Microbial data not tabulated as numbers (heatmap only).
- Table 2–3 titles say “beef”.
- Colour a* of control loin is unusually low (0.82 at week 0).
- Inoculated surface challenge may not represent internalized contamination.

## Relevance to our research question

Named HHP cycles on raw pork change both pathogens and quality in opposite directions: 500 MPa/15 min is not the same as 100–300 MPa for Salmonella/Listeria kill, WHC/shear, colour, TBARS, or VBN. Pet-food framing is a scope caveat, not a different matrix.
