## What matches

Clinic PD-cannabinoid reviews organise by **question or symptom**, not by search batch. They split motor from non-motor and plant cannabis from licensed synthetics.

## Result-presentation craft (form only)

Comparators cluster similar experiments: nabilone sleep/anxiety together; CBD motor trials together; surveys separate from RCTs. They name why a questionnaire tremor benefit is not MDS-UPDRS III. They do not write one trial, one percentage, next trial. Pass 2 of `article.md` follows that craft (rationale **(j)**). Findings from these reviews enter the article only as already-included secondary literature, not as extra patients.

## What we omit on purpose

A full IMRaD systematic-review Results section (Białoń’s search theatre). Equipment, formulation manufacturing, and health-system integration as peer `##` chapters (de Aquino’s policy spine) — no primary measurements here.

## What we should add before drafting

Condition-cluster paragraphs (same endpoint family, named differences). Added in rationale **(j)** and in pass 2.

## Verdict

The outline is field-typical for a narrative PD-cannabinoid review: teach circuits and products, then use, then defined clinical exposures, then imaging, then models. Pass 1 dumped one study per paragraph; pass 2 matches the clustering craft of the clinic comparators without importing their numbers as new trials.
