# Modulation of cannabinoid receptor 2 alters neuroinflammation and reduces formation of alpha-synuclein aggregates in a rat model of nigral synucleinopathy

- **Citation:** Joers V, Murray BC, McLaughlin C, Oliver D, Staley HE, Coronado J, Achat-Mendes C, Golshani S, Kelly SD, Goodson M, Lee D, Manfredsson FP, Moore BM II, Tansey MG. J Neuroinflammation. 2024;21:240. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12974-024-03221-5
- **Source file:** papers/2026-09-20-pd-cannabinoids/Joers2024.txt
- **Extracted:** 2026-09-20

## Research question
Does peripheral CB2 inverse agonist SMM-189 change immune phenotype and reduce phosphorylated/aggregated α-synuclein in a rat AAV nigral synucleinopathy model?

## Methods
Male Sprague-Dawley rats (16–18 weeks, 400–525 g), unilateral AAV2/5-human WT α-synuclein. Low-titer cohort (2.9–4.3 × 10^11 vg/mL; previously non-neurotoxic): from 1 week post-surgery, SMM-189 6 mg/kg i.p. daily vs vehicle for 7 weeks of dosing, 8-week follow-up. High-titer cohort (4.6 × 10^12 vg/mL) for flow cytometry at 4 and 8 weeks. IHC pSer129, proteinase-K–resistant Asyn, CD68, IBA1; striatal/nigral western blot; PBMC/brain qPCR and flow; RAW264.7 LPS + 3 µM SMM-189 pHrodo phagocytosis. Mouse PK: brain/plasma Cmax ratio 0.74 i.p. and 1.63 p.o. at 6 mg/kg.

## Sample / data
Low cohort: SMM-189 n = 12, vehicle n = 12 (n = 2/group removed for poor nigral targeting). Extra low-cohort tissue: SMM-189 n = 6, vehicle n = 3 (n = 2 removed). High cohort: 4 weeks SMM-189 n = 5 / vehicle n = 6; 8 weeks n = 7 / n = 7; blood flow n = 13 / n = 13.

## Claim-ready facts
- **Design:** preclinical in vivo (AAV-hAsyn rat) plus in vitro macrophage phagocytosis
- **Population / model:** male Sprague-Dawley rats; nigral AAV2/5-human WT α-synuclein (low titer = inflammation without planned nigral death; high titer for infiltration)
- **n:** primary IHC/western low cohort after exclusions typically ~10/group (12−2); pSer129 t(23) and t(15) imply combined/subset ns
- **Intervention / comparator:** SMM-189 6 mg/kg i.p. daily vs vehicle
- **Primary endpoint:** nigral pSer129 α-synuclein (IHC optical density normalized to vehicle; western blot)
- **Primary result:** SMM-189 reduced nigral pSer129 vs vehicle (t(23) = 3.353, p = 0.0023). Proteinase-K–resistant Asyn also reduced (p = 0.044). Western pS129 reduced (t(15) = 3.710, p = 0.0021); total human Asyn unchanged (p = 0.2133). (Figure 2)
- **Cannot show:** dopaminergic neuroprotection (low-titer model chosen not to induce nigral neurotoxicity); classical CB2 agonism; patient outcomes. DAT showed only a trend (p = 0.051).

## Key findings
- IBA1 counts (F(1,22) = 18.72, p = 0.003) and area (F(1,22) = 22.94, p < 0.0001) increased in Asyn-lesioned nigra; SMM-189 partially lessened the lesion effect (counts p = 0.0115 vs naive comparison set; vs vehicle counts p = 0.0028) (Section on anti-inflammatory environment).
- Nigral CD68 particle counts increased with SMM-189 (t(15) = 3.413, p = 0.0039); CD163 decreased (t(7) = 5.172, p = 0.0013); MHCII area not different (Figure 3).
- High cohort: delayed Asyn-induced immune infiltration (flow); PBMCs: fewer classical monocytes, more non-classical/wound-healing monocytes (Figure 6).
- LPS-stimulated RAW264.7 phagocytosis of pHrodo E. coli increased with SMM-189 (Abstract/in vitro).
- Plasma SMM-189 detectable at 4 and 8 weeks (low cohort treatment F(1,7) = 16.46, p = 0.0048); CSF levels remained low (PK text). Mouse i.p. plasma Cmax 277.07 ng/mL, brain 204.64 ng/mL (Table 2).

## Limitations
- Males only; inverse agonist, not phytocannabinoid.
- Low-titer arm is an inflammation/aggregation model, not a cell-loss PD model.
- Poor targeting exclusions; some IHC n as low as t(7).
- IBA1 cell-body count/size at 8 weeks not significantly changed by treatment (authors).
- No motor behavioral battery as a primary efficacy readout in the abstract.

## Relevance to our research question
Shows selective CB2 *inverse agonism* can lower toxic phospho-α-synuclein and push phagocytic immune programs in a PD synuclein model. Mechanism is immunomodulatory rather than direct dopaminergic rescue.

## Open questions / things to verify
- Exact percent reduction of pSer129 (only t/p reported).
- Whether high-titer animals had DA neuron loss (not the stated goal of the low cohort).
- Translation of inverse agonism vs CB2 agonists used in toxin PD models.
