# Assessing the Safety and Therapeutic Efficacy of Cannabidiol Lipid Nanoparticles in Alleviating Metabolic and Memory Impairments and Hippocampal Histopathological Changes in Diabetic Parkinson’s Rats

- **Citation:** Lapmanee S, Bhubhanil S, Wongchitrat P, Charoenphon N, Inchan A, Ngernsutivorakul T, Dechbumroong P, Khongkow M, Namdee K. Pharmaceutics. 2024;16(4):514. doi: 10.3390/pharmaceutics16040514
- **Source file:** papers/2026-09-20-pd-cannabinoids/Lapmanee2024.txt
- **Extracted:** 2026-09-20

## Research question
Are CBD lipid nanoparticles (CBD-LNP) less cytotoxic than free CBD, and do they improve metabolic, motor, memory, striatal dopamine, and hippocampal histology readouts in rats with combined high-fat/STZ diabetes plus MPTP parkinsonism versus free CBD or levodopa?

## Methods
CBD-LNP by solvent injection/microfluidization (target 3 mg/mL CBD); size, PDI, zeta, EE by HPLC. SH-SY5Y MTT, 24 h. Male Wistar rats, 8 weeks, 180–200 g, n = 8/group (N = 40): control+vehicle; DP+vehicle; DP+CBD 20 mg/kg p.o.; DP+CBD-LNP 20 mg/kg p.o.; DP+levodopa 10 mg/kg p.o. DP: HFD 1 week then STZ 35 mg/kg, diabetes if fasting glucose >180 mg/dL, then MPTP 30 mg/kg i.p. daily 4 weeks on HFD; treatments 4 weeks. Catalepsy bar (max 180 s), OFT, Y-maze, NOR; plasma lipids/glucose; HOMA-IR/HOMA-β; striatal dopamine ELISA; serum TNF-α; hippocampal H&E pyknotic index. ANOVA + Dunnett; mean ± SEM.

## Sample / data
40 rats, 8/group for behavior and biochemistry. Histology n = 3–4/group. SH-SY5Y cytotoxicity: concentrations shown in Figure 1 (numeric IC50 not stated in text). Encapsulation efficiency 98.78 ± 0.90%. CBD-LNP size 156.33 ± 1.24 nm (Day 0), PDI 0.11 ± 0.10, zeta −16.57 ± 0.04 mV.

## Claim-ready facts
- **Design:** preclinical in vitro cytotoxicity plus in vivo diabetic-MPTP rat study
- **Population / model:** male Wistar rats; STZ 35 mg/kg + HFD + MPTP 30 mg/kg × 4 weeks (“diabetic Parkinson’s”)
- **n:** 8/group (histology 3–4)
- **Intervention / comparator:** oral CBD-LNP 20 mg/kg vs free CBD 20 mg/kg vs levodopa 10 mg/kg vs vehicle
- **Primary endpoint:** motor (catalepsy descent latency) and striatal dopamine (ELISA), plus memory (Y-maze % alternation, NOR discrimination)
- **Primary result:** Vehicle DP rats had higher bar latency, fewer OFT line crosses/rearings, lower Y-maze alternation and NOR discrimination vs control (Figure 2; * to ***). CBD-LNP and levodopa significantly decreased bar latency vs DP-vehicle (†/††/†††); CBD-LNP also differed from free CBD on at least one behavior (# p < 0.05). Striatal dopamine fell in DP-vehicle and rose with CBD-LNP (and levodopa/CBD to varying degrees) vs DP-vehicle (Figure 3C; n = 8). Exact dopamine concentrations (ng or pg units) are not printed in the narrative—only directional p-coded figure results.
- **Cannot show:** human diabetic PD; that MPTP in rats is equivalent to human nigral synucleinopathy; a numeric IC50 for SH-SY5Y.

## Key findings
- CBD-LNP cytotoxicity on SH-SY5Y was “considerably lower” than isolated CBD; LNP enhanced proliferation at low concentrations (Section 3.1; Figure 1).
- DP-vehicle: higher triglycerides, cholesterol, fasting glucose, TNF-α; lower insulin, HOMA-β, striatal dopamine vs control (Figure 3).
- CBD-LNP reduced lipid profiles, increased insulin, and restored dopamine more than free CBD (Abstract; Figure 3 # vs CBD).
- Levodopa improved dopamine and some lipids but did not dominate HOMA-IR/HOMA-β vs control (Discussion).
- CBD-LNP associated with more hippocampal neuronal “migration of damaged cells” recovery than levodopa (Abstract); pyknotic index quantified in histology (n = 3–4).

## Limitations
- Combined STZ+MPTP+HFD confounds metabolic vs nigrostriatal effects.
- Male rats only; oral 20 mg/kg without plasma CBD PK in this paper.
- Primary numeric endpoints live in figures, not tables in the extracted text.
- Histology n = 3–4.
- Authors note residual biochemical abnormalities vs healthy controls after all treatments.

## Relevance to our research question
Direct CBD (free vs nano) in an MPTP-based PD toxin model, with levodopa comparator and dopamine ELISA. Formulation changes efficacy. Diabetes comorbidity is extra-scope but clinically adjacent.
