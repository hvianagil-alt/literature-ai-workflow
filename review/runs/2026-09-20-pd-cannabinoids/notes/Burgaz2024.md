# Investigation in the cannabigerol derivative VCE-003.2 as a disease-modifying agent in a mouse model of experimental synucleinopathy

- **Citation:** Burgaz S, Navarro E, Rodríguez-Carreiro S, Navarrete C, Garrido-Rodríguez M, Lastres-Becker I, Chocarro J, Lanciego JL, Muñoz E, Fernández-Ruiz J. Behav Brain Funct. 2024;20:28. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12993-024-00256-9
- **Source file:** papers/2026-09-20-pd-cannabinoids/Burgaz2024.txt
- **Extracted:** 2026-09-20

## Research question
Does oral VCE-003.2 (a PPAR-γ–active cannabigerol quinone derivative without CB1/CB2 activity) protect motor function and nigral TH neurons in mice with AAV9-driven A53T α-synuclein overexpression?

## Methods
Unilateral SNpc AAV9-SynA53T (or AAV9-null sham). Daily oral VCE-003.2 20 mg/kg or sesame-oil vehicle for 14 days starting ~24 h after vector. Cylinder/rearing, elevated body-swing, actimetry; TH IHC plus Aiforia deep-learning neuron counts; CD68, GFAP, α-synuclein; striatal RNA-seq. ANOVA + Tukey. Separate ADMET/transporter assays on the compound.

## Sample / data
Adult male mice. Group sizes: vehicle sham 4–6; vehicle AAV9-SynA53T 8–10; VCE-003.2 AAV9-SynA53T 8–10 (ranges after outlier removal). Figure legends: “more than 4” or “more than 5” animals/group. F-tests imply ~18–21 residual df depending on endpoint.

## Claim-ready facts
- **Design:** preclinical in vivo (viral A53T synucleinopathy; oral CBG derivative)
- **Population / model:** adult male mice; unilateral SNpc AAV9-SynA53T
- **n:** sham 4–6; lesion groups 8–10 (exact n varies by assay)
- **Intervention / comparator:** VCE-003.2 20 mg/kg p.o. × 14 days vs vehicle; sham AAV9-null
- **Primary endpoint:** motor laterality (cylinder preference) and SNpc TH immunoreactivity / TH+ neuron number
- **Primary result:** Cylinder ipsilateral-paw preference after AAV9-SynA53T was prevented by VCE-003.2 (F(2,17) = 11.50, p < 0.001). Vehicle AAV mice lost ~35% nigral TH immunoreactivity (F(2,21) = 20.67, p < 0.0001); VCE-003.2 restored TH intensity to sham-like values (Tukey ### p < 0.005 vs vehicle AAV). Deep-learning TH+ neuron counts: F(2,21) = 6.72, p < 0.01. (Figures 2–3)
- **Cannot show:** CB1/CB2-mediated effects (compound described as PPAR-γ partial agonist, no CB1/CB2 activity); human disease modification; EBST laterality was not rescued.

## Key findings
- Elevated-body-swing contralateral preference was not attenuated by VCE-003.2 (F(2,19) = 1.49, ns); rearing trend F(2,18) = 2.99, p = 0.075 (Section “Behavioural readouts”).
- Actimetry: VCE-003.2 increased general activity, ambulation, and velocities vs vehicle AAV (several F(2,19) p < 0.05) (same section).
- Microgliosis CD68 F(2,17) = 27.58, p < 0.0001; astrogliosis GFAP F(2,21) = 26.17, p < 0.0001; VCE-003.2 reduced both vs vehicle AAV (Figure 4).
- RNA-seq: pathology changed mitochondrial, lysosomal, immune, and lipid genes; VCE-003.2 mainly affected immune/interferon signaling (Abstract/transcriptomic section).
- Oral ADMET described as favorable; ABC transporter inhibition at 22.5 µM (BCRP 89%, MDR1 81%); SLC transporters not inhibited (Supplementary File 2).

## Limitations
- Male mice; 2-week AAV phenotype is compressed vs human PD.
- Group n after “statistical outlier” removal is a range, not a fixed n.
- EBST did not improve; cylinder and TH did.
- PPAR-γ dependence in *this* A53T experiment is inferred from prior 6-OHDA/LPS work, not a co-administered PPAR antagonist here.

## Relevance to our research question
Cannabinoid-related (CBG-derived) oral agent in a PD *synuclein* model, complementary to 6-OHDA/LPS. Shows motor and TH protection with reduced gliosis.

## Open questions / things to verify
