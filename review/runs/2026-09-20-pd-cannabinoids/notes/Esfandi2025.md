# Cannabidiol Extracted from Cannabis sativa L. Plant Shows Neuroprotective Impacts Against 6-HODA-Induced Neurotoxicity via Nrf2 Signal Transduction Pathway

- **Citation:** Esfandi A, Mehrafarin A, Kalateh Jari S, Naghdi Badi H, Larijani K. Iran J Pharm Res. 2025;24(1):e160499. https://doi.org/10.5812/ijpr-160499
- **Source file:** papers/2026-09-20-pd-cannabinoids/Esfandi2025.txt
- **Extracted:** 2026-09-20

## Research question
Does plant-extracted cannabidiol (CBD) protect PC12 cells from 6-hydroxydopamine (6-OHDA/6-HODA)-induced neurotoxicity, and is that protection linked to the Nrf2 antioxidant pathway?

## Methods
CBD was extracted from greenhouse-grown hemp (microwave-dried; UHPLC/UV). PC12 cells were pretreated with CBD (1, 5, or 10 µM) then exposed to 100 µM 6-OHDA for 24 h. Endpoints: MTT viability, flow-cytometry apoptosis (V-FITC/PI), DCF-DA ROS, TBARS MDA, FRAP total antioxidant capacity, kit SOD and GSH, and RT-PCR of Nrf2, Bax, Bcl-2, and Casp3 (2−ΔΔCT; GAPDH). ANOVA plus Tukey; P < 0.05.

## Sample / data
In vitro PC12 (Pasteur Institute of Iran). Viability, apoptosis, MDA, ROS, TAC, SOD, GSH: n = 4 per condition. Gene expression: n = 3. No animals.

## Claim-ready facts
- **Design:** preclinical in vitro (toxin-exposed neuronal cell line)
- **Population / model:** PC12 cells + 100 µM 6-OHDA (authors’ PD-like neurotoxicity model)
- **n:** 4 independent wells/replicates for viability and oxidative assays; 3 for qPCR
- **Intervention / comparator:** CBD 1, 5, 10 µM pretreatment vs ethanol/saline vehicle vs 6-OHDA alone
- **Primary endpoint:** cell viability (% MTT) and apoptosis (%) after 6-OHDA
- **Primary result:** 6-OHDA reduced viability to 48.25 ± 6.65% vs control 97.25 ± 2.21% (P < 0.0001); 5 µM CBD 69.25 ± 3.50% (P < 0.001 vs 6-OHDA) and 10 µM CBD 81.65 ± 4.50% (P < 0.0001); 5 vs 10 µM P = 0.029. Apoptosis: 6-OHDA 36.82 ± 5.81% vs control 1.5 ± 1.29% (P < 0.0001); 5 µM CBD 18.04 ± 4.05% and 10 µM 12.60 ± 2.07% (significant vs 6-OHDA). 1 µM CBD did not significantly change MDA vs 6-OHDA (P = 0.128). (Figure 1; n = 4)
- **Cannot show:** in vivo PD, motor symptoms, dopaminergic nigral loss, or clinical CBD efficacy; Nrf2 was measured as mRNA only, not protein/nuclear translocation.

## Key findings
- 10 µM CBD raised FRAP TAC from 19.25 ± 4.11 µM (6-OHDA) toward 39.75 ± 3.50 µM vs 5 µM 25.30 ± 4.03 µM (5 vs 10 µM P = 0.039); control TAC 50.25 ± 5.90 µM (Section 4.3).
- 6-OHDA raised MDA to 42.00 ± 3.65 µM and ROS to 228 ± 0.38% of control; 5 and 10 µM CBD lowered MDA (P = 0.003 and P < 0.0001) and ROS (P = 0.014 and P < 0.0001) (Section 4.2; n = 4).
- 10 µM CBD improved SOD (P = 0.009) and GSH (P = 0.010) vs 5 µM (Section 4.3).
- 5 and 10 µM CBD increased Nrf2 and Bcl-2 mRNA and reduced Bax and Casp3 vs 6-OHDA (Figure 4; n = 3).

## Limitations
- Single cell line; 6-OHDA in vitro is not a complete PD model.
- Title uses “6-HODA”; methods use 6-OHDA — same toxin, spelling inconsistency.
- Extraction yield 431.3 ± 1.25 µg/g is from a prior paper, not this experiment’s bioassay dose.
- No CB1/CB2 pharmacology; mechanism inferred from Nrf2/apoptosis transcripts.
- Authors do not report a dedicated limitations section.

## Relevance to our research question
Directly tests CBD against a classic PD catecholaminergic toxin (6-OHDA) and reports concentration-dependent rescue of viability with antioxidant/Nrf2 readouts. Supports cannabinoid neuroprotection in a toxin model, not patients.
