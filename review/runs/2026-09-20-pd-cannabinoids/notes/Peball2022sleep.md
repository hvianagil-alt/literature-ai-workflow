# Effects of Nabilone on Sleep Outcomes in Patients with Parkinson's Disease: A Post-hoc Analysis of the NMS-Nab Trial

- **Citation:** Peball M, Seppi K, Krismer F, et al. Movement Disorders Clinical Practice. 2022. doi:10.1002/mdc3.13471. NCT03769896.
- **Source file:** papers/2026-09-20-pd-cannabinoids-gapfill/Peball2022sleep.txt
- **Extracted:** 2026-09-20
- **Gap-fill:** g1 — placebo-controlled nabilone sleep outcomes (parent RCT of the 2024 open-label extension)

## Research question
In people with PD who had clinically relevant sleep problems at baseline, does nabilone change MDS-UPDRS night-time sleep and NMSS Sleep/Fatigue scores during open-label treatment and during randomized withdrawal versus placebo?

## Methods
Post-hoc analysis of the NMS-Nab enriched-enrollment randomized-withdrawal trial. Sleep-symptomatic subset defined as MDS-UPDRS item 1.7 ≥2 at baseline. Outcomes: MDS-UPDRS 1.7/1.8, NMSS Domain 2 and items Q3–Q6, ESS. Open-label nabilone then double-blind continuation versus placebo.

## Sample / data
31 of 38 randomized PD patients had MDS-UPDRS-1.7 ≥2 at baseline. Sleep problems were not an inclusion criterion of the parent trial.

## Claim-ready facts
- **Design:** Post-hoc analysis of a placebo-controlled, double-blind, enriched-enrollment randomized-withdrawal RCT
- **Population / model:** People with PD and disturbing NMS who also had clinically relevant night-time sleep problems (MDS-UPDRS-1.7 ≥2)
- **n:** 31 sleep-symptomatic of 38 randomized
- **Intervention / comparator:** Oral nabilone (open-label then continued) vs placebo withdrawal
- **Primary endpoint:** MDS-UPDRS-1.7 and NMSS Domain 2 (Sleep/Fatigue)
- **Primary result:** Open-label nabilone: MDS-UPDRS-1.7 and NMSS-D2 improved (P<0.001); 24/31 (77.4%) had no relevant sleep problem (1.7 ≤1) at randomization; 30/31 (96.8%) improved ≥1 point. Double-blind: placebo MDS-UPDRS-1.7 worsened 2.00 points (95% CI 1.32–2.68; P=0.004; ES 1.70) while nabilone 0.12 (P=1.000); between-groups P<0.001. Placebo NMSS-D2 worsened 9.57 (3.24–15.90; P=0.004) vs nabilone between-group difference 8.57 (2.24–14.91). Insomnia (NMSS Q5) drove much of the withdrawal worsening.
- **Cannot show:** Pre-specified sleep primary endpoint (post-hoc); RBD polysomnography; CBD comparison.

## Key findings
- Daytime sleepiness (ESS / MDS-UPDRS-1.8) was not the main driver of benefit (Abstract/Results).
- Restless-legs NMSS Q6 improved in open-label (P=0.043).

## Limitations
- Post-hoc subset; sleep not an inclusion criterion.
- Enriched responders.
- No PSG.

## Relevance to our research question
Supplies the missing placebo-controlled sleep contrast for nabilone that the 2024 open-label extension cannot show.
