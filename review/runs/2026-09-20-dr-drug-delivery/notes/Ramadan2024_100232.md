# Development and optimization of vildagliptin solid lipid nanoparticles loaded ocuserts for controlled ocular delivery: A promising approach towards treating diabetic retinopathy

- **Citation (as given in the paper / filename):** Ramadan AEH, Elsayed MMA, Elsayed A, Fouad MA, Mohamed MS, Lee S, Mahmoud RA, Sabry SA, Ghoneim MM, Hassan AHE, Abd Elkarim RA, Belal A, El-Shenawy AA. Int J Pharm X. 2024;7:100232. doi:10.1016/j.ijpx.2024.100232
- **Source file:** papers/2026-09-20-dr-drug-delivery-found/Ramadan2024_100232.txt
- **Extracted:** 2026-09-20
- **Screening:** Included (original formulation: vildagliptin SLN ocuserts aimed at DR; no retinal efficacy arm)

## Research question
Can vildagliptin (VLD), a hydrophilic DPP-4 inhibitor, be loaded into stearic-acid solid lipid nanoparticles (SLNPs) and then into dual-coated ocuserts so that ocular residence and 24-hour release are prolonged, offering a topical alternative to oral VLD for diabetic retinopathy?

## Methods
SLNPs by double-emulsion/melt dispersion (stearic acid, Span 80, Tween 80, VLD 50 mg; drug:lipid 1:10 or 1:20; 75 °C then 4 °C quench). Eight SLNP runs characterized for yield, DLS size/PDI, zeta, HPLC EE%, TDC, and dialysis dissolution in PBS pH 7.4, 500 mL, 37 °C, 24 h. Run 3 incorporated into ocuserts (solvent casting) with a Box–Behnken design on HPMC, PVA, and Eudragit RL100 (rate-controlling membrane). Ocusert tests: weight, thickness, surface pH, folding endurance, drug loading, moisture, swelling, ex vivo mucoadhesion time, in vitro release kinetics. Optimized VSO-0: DSC, UV sterility, in vivo residual-drug release in New Zealand albino rabbits (n=24; 8 time-point groups of 3), Draize irritation (n=5), ICH accelerated stability 40 ± 1 °C / 75 ± 3% RH for 6 months. Ethics ZA-AS/PH/19/C/2023 (Methods).

## Sample / data
Eight SLNP runs; 15 OCU batches plus optimized VSO-0. In vitro n=3 unless stated. In vivo release: 24 rabbits (3 per time point at 1, 2, 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24 h). Irritation n=5 rabbits (2.4 ± 0.2 kg). No diabetic animals.

## Claim-ready facts
- **Design:** formulation optimization (Box–Behnken) plus rabbit ocular release/irritation; not a DR efficacy study
- **Population / model:** physicochemical SLNP/ocusert characterization; New Zealand albino rabbits 2.1–2.7 kg for in vivo remaining-drug release; separate Draize cohort
- **n:** SLNP/OCU assays n=3; in vivo release n=24 (8 groups × 3); irritation n=5
- **Intervention / comparator:** VLD-SLNPs (Run 3 selected for ocuserts) vs other SLNP runs and pure VLD in dissolution; optimized VSO-0 ocusert (50.303 mg HPMC, 60 mg PVA, 150 mg Eudragit RL100) in the right conjunctival sac vs left untreated eye; no anti-VEGF or steroid comparator
- **Primary endpoint:** ocusert drug loading (%) and cumulative % VLD released at 24 h in vitro and in vivo (residual drug in recovered insert); Draize score 0–9
- **Primary result:** SLNP size range 122.4 ± 1.09 nm (Run 4) to 281.9 ± 4.50 nm (Run 5); PDI 0.120–0.426; zeta −23.2 ± 1.48 to −15.4 ± 2.67 mV; EE% 18.84 ± 2.62 (Run 5) to 57.21 ± 1.32 (Run 4); 24 h SLNP dissolution 27.63 ± 2.68% (Run 7) to 69.31 ± 2.98% (Run 2) (Table 4). Selected Run 3: yield 64.02 ± 2.33%, size 137.8 ± 2.43 nm, PDI 0.256 ± 0.10, zeta −18.1 ± 1.60 mV, EE% 43.44 ± 3.17%, 24 h dissolution 57.04 ± 4.02%. Optimized VSO-0 loading 95.28 ± 2.87% (Abstract/Conclusion; Results 3.5.3 prints 95.28 ± 2.78). In vitro VSO-0 release 36.89 ± 3.11% at 24 h; in vivo remaining-drug release 35.12 ± 2.47% at 24 h (n=3 rabbits at the 24 h time point). Draize scores 0 except day-1 increased tears and eyelid swelling scored 1; days 0, 3, 5, 7 all 0 (Table 6). OCU in vitro 24 h release across batches 23.21 ± 2.18% (VSO-8) to 56.23 ± 3.63% (VSO-5); zero-order kinetics (Results).
- **Cannot show:** retinal VLD levels, retinal blood flow, VEGF, leakage, histopathology, or any DR/DME efficacy; systemic glucose after ocular dosing was not reported here

## Key findings
- Authors selected Run 3 “based on the lowest particle size, higher EE, and lowest cumulative % dissolved after 24 h” (3.2.5); Table 4 shows Run 4 actually smaller (122.4 nm) and higher EE (57.21%) with higher 24 h dissolution (64.55% vs 57.04%).
- Ocusert weight 28.54 ± 0.666 to 31.84 ± 0.443 mg; thickness 0.42 ± 0.05 to 0.76 ± 0.04 mm; surface pH 6.72 ± 0.26 to 7.37 ± 0.15 (Table 5).
- Drug loading of OCU batches 88.15 ± 1.15% (VSO-7) to 97.72 ± 2.54% (VSO-12).
- Eudragit RL100 concentration significantly increased ex vivo mucoadhesion time and decreased 24 h release (p<0.05); HPMC decreased release (p<0.05); PVA effect on MAT and release not significant (p>0.05) (3.4).
- DSC: VLD melting endotherm at 155.7 °C absent in SLNPs and VSO-0 (amorphous dispersion) (3.5.1).
- Sterility: no turbidity or growth after UV. Accelerated stability: nonsignificant change in appearance or loading (p>0.05); overall degradation <1% at 6 months (3.5.6).
- **Mechanism grade:** improved retinal microvascular flow is **cited from earlier VLD clinical/oral work**, **not measured** in this paper. Ocular delivery is supported by insert residual-drug release only.

## Limitations (as stated by the authors, or evident from the methods)
- No DR animal model and no retinal endpoint despite the DR framing in the Abstract and Conclusion.
- SLNP zeta (−15 to −23 mV) is below the authors’ own −30 mV electrostatic-stability rule; they invoke Tween 80 steric stabilization instead.
- In vivo “release” is disappearance of VLD from the recovered ocusert, not aqueous humor or vitreous concentration.
- Irritation n=5; day-1 tearing/swelling attributed to residual ethyl alcohol.
- Ranking-order selection of Run 3 is not identical to the smallest size / highest EE in Table 4.

## Relevance to our research question
Shows a topical, once-daily-intended SLNP ocusert for a small-molecule (not anti-VEGF or corticosteroid) proposed for DR. It addresses duration at the ocular surface (≈35% released in 24 h) but does not demonstrate targeting of the retina or translation beyond rabbit irritation/release.

## Open questions / things to verify
- Loading SD 2.87% (Abstract, Conclusion) vs 2.78% (Results 3.5.3); both share the 95.28 point estimate.
- Mucoadhesion time (minutes) for VSO-0 is discussed via response surfaces but a single optimized MAT number is not stated in the body text.
- Whether any VLD reached the posterior segment is unknown.
