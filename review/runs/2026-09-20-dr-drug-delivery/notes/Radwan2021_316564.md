# Hyaluronic-Coated Albumin Nanoparticles for the Non-Invasive Delivery of Apatinib in Diabetic Retinopathy

- **Citation (as given in the paper / filename):** Radwan SE-S, El-Kamel A, Zaki EI, Burgalassi S, Zucchetti E, El-Moslemany RM. Int J Nanomedicine. 2021;16:4481–4494. doi:10.2147/IJN.S316564
- **Source file:** papers/2026-09-20-dr-drug-delivery-found/Radwan2021_316564.pdf (text sidecar `.txt`)
- **Extracted:** 2026-09-20
- **Screening:** Included (hinge original study: topical HA-coated BSA nanoparticles of the VEGFR-2 inhibitor apatinib in a streptozotocin DR rat model)

## Research question
Can hyaluronic acid–coated bovine serum albumin nanoparticles loaded with apatinib (Apa-HA-BSA-NPs) deliver this VEGFR-2 inhibitor to the retina after topical instillation and treat experimental diabetic retinopathy as well as a single intravitreal injection, without corneal cytotoxicity?

## Methods
Desolvation of BSA (pH 8, ethanol, 25 µL of 8% v/v glutaraldehyde, 24 h) with Apa (5 mg/mL in DMSO) added to the BSA solution; HA coating by adjusting NP pH to the BSA isoelectric point (4.9) and adding HA (2 mg/mL selected). Colloidal properties by DLS/zeta; TEM; HPLC-UV EE%; dialysis release in PBS pH 7.4 at 37 °C, 100 rpm, 0.25–24 h (n=3); mucin viscosity and zeta at 1:1 mix; WST-1 on rabbit corneal epithelial (RCE) cells after 30 min. In vivo: male Wistar rats, 200–250 g; two intraperitoneal STZ doses of 65 mg/kg; 6 weeks of hyperglycemia (inclusion if glucose consistently >170 mg/dL); six groups n=5 (normal control; DR positive control; single 10 µL IVT Apa-BSA-NPs or Apa-HA-BSA-NPs in the right eye; topical 50 µL of the same formulations twice daily for 2 weeks). Left eyes untreated. After treatment, blood sugar kept ≤350 mg/dL. Endpoints: H&E retinal thickness (RT) and TEM capillary basement-membrane (BM) thickness (ImageJ, average of 20 measurements); DiD-labeled NP confocal retinal mounts 5 h after a single 10 µL IVT (left) plus 50 µL topical (right) dose in normal and diabetic rats, groups of three. Statistics: mean ± SD; t-test; p≤0.05 (Methods; Results).

## Sample / data
Formulation characterization n=3. Efficacy histopathology n=5 rats per group. Morphometry n=20 measurements (Figure 3B legend). Confocal biodistribution groups of three. RCE cytotoxicity: 3×10^5 cells/well (Methods).

## Claim-ready facts
- **Design:** preclinical formulation plus in vivo efficacy/biodistribution study (not a clinical trial)
- **Population / model:** male Wistar rats with STZ-induced diabetic retinopathy (glucose >170 mg/dL after 6 weeks); RCE cells for cytotoxicity; PBS/mucin for physicochemical tests
- **n:** efficacy groups n=5; confocal groups of 3; in vitro characterization n=3; morphometry averaged from 20 measurements (Figure 3B)
- **Intervention / comparator:** selected Apa-HA-BSA-NP2 (50 mg BSA, 2 mg HA) vs uncoated Apa-BSA-NPs vs untreated DR vs normal control; topical 50 µL twice daily × 14 days vs single 10 µL IVT; milligram Apa dose per eye not reported
- **Primary endpoint:** retinal histopathology and morphometry — full retinal thickness (RT, µm) and capillary basement-membrane thickness (BM, µm) on day 14; supporting confocal mean corrected total fluorescence 5 h after a single dose
- **Primary result:** Selected HA-coated particles: size 222.2 ± 3.56 nm, PdI 0.22 (abstract 0.221 ± 0.02), zeta −37.3 ± 1.8 mV, EE% 69 ± 1%; uncoated Apa-BSA-NPs 198.9 ± 3.52 nm, zeta −29.2 ± 1.3 mV (Table 1). In vitro release at 24 h: 98% uncoated vs 66.67% HA-coated (biphasic; n=3). RCE viability remained >95% at all BSA-NP concentrations tested. DR control RT 220.24 µm vs normal 151.84 µm; topical Apa-HA-BSA-NP RT 158.97 µm, p>0.05 vs normal control, and significantly lower vs DR control; IVT formulations produced subnormal RT. All treatments decreased BM thickness vs DR control (p<0.05); HA-coated NPs (topical or IVT) reached BM 0.09 µm, comparable to normal control (Figure 3B). In diabetic rats, topical HA-coated vs uncoated fluorescence 12.28 ± 1.39 vs 3.55 ± 0.81; topical HA-coated intensity was 4.3 ± 0.48-fold vs similarly treated normal rats and 44% of the IVT HA-coated signal after one dose (Figure 5).
- **Cannot show:** human DR/DME efficacy, visual acuity or OCT, vitreous/retinal Apa concentration, CD44 uptake as a directly measured in vivo mechanism, or a milligram-level Apa dose–response

## Key findings
- Blank BSA-NPs at 50 mg/mL BSA: 212.0 ± 0.35 nm, PdI 0.21, zeta −29.5 ± 0.05 mV; chosen as the protein concentration for loading (Table 1).
- At pH 4.9, uncoated Apa-BSA-NP zeta shifted from −29.2 ± 1.3 to +6.03 ± 2.1 mV before HA coating; unbound HA after coating at 2 mg/mL was 2.4% by CTAB assay (Results).
- Mucoadhesion: HA-BSA-NP viscosity 19.01 ± 2.3 cP vs BSA-NP 5.05 ± 0.3 cP; after mucin, 28.42 ± 1.25 vs 12.79 ± 0.38 cP; mucin control 10.5 ± 0.2 cP. Zeta change after mucin was 25.4 mV for HA-BSA-NPs vs 5.7 mV for uncoated (Figure 2).
- Light microscopy: untreated DR retina had more dilated/congested vessels; uncoated NPs (topical or IVT) had fewer vessels; HA-coated groups looked comparable to normal vascularization (Figure 3A).
- TEM: HA-coated topical and IVT groups showed almost normal photoreceptor ultrastructure and normal vessel BM; uncoated topical showed more residual distortion than uncoated IVT (Figure 4).
- Confocal, normal rats: no group difference, mean fluorescence 2.82 ± 0.67. Diabetic IVT uncoated and HA-coated increased 7.93 ± 0.93-fold and 9.42 ± 1.62-fold vs normal; topical uncoated did not show a significant DR-related increase, only HA-coated topical did (Figure 5).
- **Mechanism grade:** HA–CD44 receptor-mediated uptake is **proposed** from prior literature and from higher HA-coated fluorescence; CD44 occupancy was **not directly measured** here. Negative zeta and ~200 nm size are **interpreted** (not newly mapped) as allowing vitreous diffusion (Discussion).

## Limitations (as stated by the authors, or evident from the methods)
- Authors report no conflicts; they do not list a numbered limitations section.
- n=5 per efficacy arm; morphometry is 20 measurements, not 20 animals.
- Primary in vivo readout is histology/TEM, not electroretinography, OCT, or fluorescein leakage.
- Apa concentration in the instilled NP dispersion (mg/mL or µg/eye) is not stated; only volumes are given.
- Topical regimen is twice daily for 14 days vs a single IVT injection, so “comparable efficacy” is not an equal-dose comparison.
- STZ DR model (glucose >170 mg/dL) is not a DME clinical population; no human data.

## Relevance to our research question
Hinge original evidence that a topical HA-coated albumin nanocarrier can place a small-molecule VEGFR-2 inhibitor in the diabetic rat retina and normalize RT/BM toward control, approaching a single IVT dose after repeated drops. It speaks to non-invasive targeting of the posterior segment and to duration (24 h in vitro release; 2-week topical course) but does not measure anti-VEGF protein drugs or clinical DME.
