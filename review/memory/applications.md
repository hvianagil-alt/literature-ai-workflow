# What “good” looks like — by use

The article is always Markdown. These rows change **emphasis and length**, not honesty.

| What they said | Default length | Extraction lean | Methods depth | Extra |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Journal article / “just write the review” | ≥ ~6,000 words body | findings **and** methods | brief narrative Methods | full tables; teach in Introduction |
| Thesis chapter | same floor unless they ask shorter | methods-heavy if they say so | can expand eligibility | keep their research question in the aim paragraph |
| Grant background | same floor or a short note if they ask | findings and gaps | short | Discussion must name the next measurement that would change the grant |
| Paper introduction only | they may ask `--short` | findings | very short Methods | still teach; still no invented citations |
| Personal reading / “I am new to this topic” | full narrative unless they ask for a table only | findings | short | Introduction must assume no subfield knowledge |
| Table only | stop after literature table | — | — | still run extraction checks; do not skip the first conversation |

If they say “just go”: journal-style narrative; last 6 years for related search; peer-reviewed journals; Markdown now; Word/PDF only if they later ask.
